CELPIP Speaking Task 4: Making Predictions

1 Everyday Mishaps and Problems

Topics: spills, delays, forgotten items, a car problem, a missed bus, something about to break.

General Template

Based on what I see in the picture, [the situation and the clue]. I think that [prediction].

As a result, [first consequence]. After that, [what the people will do or feel].

Otherwise, [an alternative outcome]. In the end, [final result].

1.1 Stack of Boxes

A man carrying a tall stack of boxes toward a door while a child runs in front of him. What do you think will happen next?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a man is carrying a tall stack of boxes, and he can’t see where he’s going. A little girl is running right in front of him.

I think that he will probably bump into her. As a result, the boxes will fall to the ground, and the child may get scared and start to cry. After that, the man will put down what’s left, apologize, and check that she’s not hurt.

Otherwise, if he notices her in time, he’ll stop and step aside. In the end, I believe he will learn to carry fewer boxes at once, and the child’s parent will keep a closer eye on her.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
tall stack a high pile of things He carried a tall stack of books.
step aside move out of the way Please step aside for the cart.
bump into to accidentally hit someone or something I bumped into a table in the dark.

1.2 Missing the Bus

A woman rushing to a bus stop as the bus is pulling away. What do you think will happen?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a woman is running with a bag over her shoulder, and she’s waving at a bus that is already leaving the stop. She looks desperate.

I think that the driver might not notice her, so she will miss the bus. As a result, she’ll be late for work or an appointment. After that, she’ll probably check the schedule for the next bus or call a taxi.

Otherwise, if the driver sees her in the mirror, he might stop and wait. In the end, I think she’ll catch her breath and make a note to leave home earlier tomorrow to avoid the stress.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
desperate in great need and willing to try anything He gave a desperate wave.
catch her breath rest until breathing is normal She stopped to catch her breath.
avoid stay away from I avoid rush hour.

1.3 Waiter and Chair

A waiter carrying several plates near a table where a customer is leaning back in his chair. Predict what will happen.

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a waiter is balancing four plates on his arms, and he’s walking behind a customer who is leaning back in his chair.

I think that the chair will hit the waiter, and he’ll lose his balance. As a result, the plates will crash onto the floor, and the food will spill all over the customers. After that, the whole restaurant will turn to look, and the manager will rush over to clean up.

Otherwise, the waiter might notice in time and swerve around the chair. In the end, I think the manager will apologize and offer a free dessert, and the customer will be more careful.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
leaning back tilting backward He was leaning back on two legs of the chair.
swerve turn suddenly to avoid something The driver swerved to miss the dog.
free dessert a sweet dish given without charge They offered a free dessert.

1.4 Flat Tire

A driver with a flat tire on the side of the road, and a tow truck in the distance. What will happen next?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a driver is standing beside his car on a quiet road, and the back tire is completely flat. In the distance, a tow truck is coming closer.

I think that the tow truck will stop, and the operator will offer to help. As a result, he’ll probably change the tire quickly or lift the car onto the truck if the damage is bad. After that, the driver will thank him and pay for the service.

Otherwise, the truck may be on its way to another emergency and drive past. In the end, the driver will get back on the road, but he’ll be running late and will probably have the tires checked.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
tow truck a vehicle that pulls broken cars A tow truck took our car away.
emergency a sudden dangerous situation There was an emergency on the highway.
running late behind schedule I’m running late for work.
checked examined to make sure it is fine I had my brakes checked.

A cyclist approaching a puddle next to pedestrians on the sidewalk. Predict what will happen.

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a cyclist is riding fast along a rainy street, and there’s a big puddle just ahead. A few pedestrians are walking close to the edge of the sidewalk.

I think that the cyclist will ride straight through the puddle. As a result, water will splash up and soak the pedestrians’ clothes. After that, they’ll probably shout angrily, and the cyclist will feel embarrassed and apologize.

Otherwise, if he sees the puddle early, he might slow down or go around it. In the end, I think everyone will move on, but the pedestrians will stay further back from the road, and the cyclist will be more considerate next time.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
puddle a small pool of water I stepped in a puddle.
apologize say sorry He apologized for the mess.
stay further back keep a greater distance Please stay further back from the edge.
considerate thoughtful about others A considerate driver slows down.

Otherwise, if the storm hits before they reach safety, they’ll look for a shelter among the trees. In the end, I think they’ll return safely, tired and wet, and they’ll plan the next trip with a better weather forecast.

Otherwise, if the sun hides again, the snowman will survive until the evening. In the end, I think the kids will rebuild it on a colder day, and they’ll take a photo to preserve the memory.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence

preserve keep safe A photo preserves a memory.
