CELPIP Speaking Task 4: Making Predictions

1 Everyday Mishaps and Problems

Topics: spills, delays, forgotten items, a car problem, a missed bus, something about to break.

General Template

Based on what I see in the picture, [the situation and the clue]. I think that [prediction].

As a result, [first consequence]. After that, [what the people will do or feel].

Otherwise, [an alternative outcome]. In the end, [final result].

1.1 Stack of Boxes

A man carrying a tall stack of boxes toward a door while a child runs in front of him. What do you think will happen next?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a man is carrying a tall stack of boxes, and he can’t see where he’s going. A little girl is running right in front of him.

I think that he will probably bump into her. As a result, the boxes will fall to the ground, and the child may get scared and start to cry. After that, the man will put down what’s left, apologize, and check that she’s not hurt.

Otherwise, if he notices her in time, he’ll stop and step aside. In the end, I believe he will learn to carry fewer boxes at once, and the child’s parent will keep a closer eye on her.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
tall stack a high pile of things He carried a tall stack of books.
step aside move out of the way Please step aside for the cart.
bump into to accidentally hit someone or something I bumped into a table in the dark.

1.2 Missing the Bus

A woman rushing to a bus stop as the bus is pulling away. What do you think will happen?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a woman is running with a bag over her shoulder, and she’s waving at a bus that is already leaving the stop. She looks desperate.

I think that the driver might not notice her, so she will miss the bus. As a result, she’ll be late for work or an appointment. After that, she’ll probably check the schedule for the next bus or call a taxi.

Otherwise, if the driver sees her in the mirror, he might stop and wait. In the end, I think she’ll catch her breath and make a note to leave home earlier tomorrow to avoid the stress.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
desperate in great need and willing to try anything He gave a desperate wave.
catch her breath rest until breathing is normal She stopped to catch her breath.
avoid stay away from I avoid rush hour.

1.3 Waiter and Chair

A waiter carrying several plates near a table where a customer is leaning back in his chair. Predict what will happen.

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a waiter is balancing four plates on his arms, and he’s walking behind a customer who is leaning back in his chair.

I think that the chair will hit the waiter, and he’ll lose his balance. As a result, the plates will crash onto the floor, and the food will spill all over the customers. After that, the whole restaurant will turn to look, and the manager will rush over to clean up.

Otherwise, the waiter might notice in time and swerve around the chair. In the end, I think the manager will apologize and offer a free dessert, and the customer will be more careful.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
leaning back tilting backward He was leaning back on two legs of the chair.
swerve turn suddenly to avoid something The driver swerved to miss the dog.
free dessert a sweet dish given without charge They offered a free dessert.

1.4 Flat Tire

A driver with a flat tire on the side of the road, and a tow truck in the distance. What will happen next?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a driver is standing beside his car on a quiet road, and the back tire is completely flat. In the distance, a tow truck is coming closer.

I think that the tow truck will stop, and the operator will offer to help. As a result, he’ll probably change the tire quickly or lift the car onto the truck if the damage is bad. After that, the driver will thank him and pay for the service.

Otherwise, the truck may be on its way to another emergency and drive past. In the end, the driver will get back on the road, but he’ll be running late and will probably have the tires checked.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
tow truck a vehicle that pulls broken cars A tow truck took our car away.
emergency a sudden dangerous situation There was an emergency on the highway.
running late behind schedule I’m running late for work.
checked examined to make sure it is fine I had my brakes checked.

1.5 Cyclist and Puddle

A cyclist approaching a puddle next to pedestrians on the sidewalk. Predict what will happen.

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a cyclist is riding fast along a rainy street, and there’s a big puddle just ahead. A few pedestrians are walking close to the edge of the sidewalk.

I think that the cyclist will ride straight through the puddle. As a result, water will splash up and soak the pedestrians’ clothes. After that, they’ll probably shout angrily, and the cyclist will feel embarrassed and apologize.

Otherwise, if he sees the puddle early, he might slow down or go around it. In the end, I think everyone will move on, but the pedestrians will stay further back from the road, and the cyclist will be more considerate next time.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
puddle a small pool of water I stepped in a puddle.
apologize say sorry He apologized for the mess.
stay further back keep a greater distance Please stay further back from the edge.
considerate thoughtful about others A considerate driver slows down.

2 Weather and Outdoor Plans

Topics: dark clouds, a picnic, a sports game, an outdoor wedding, a beach day, hiking.

General Template

Based on what I see in the picture, [the situation and the clue]. I think that [prediction].

As a result, [first consequence]. After that, [what the people will do or feel].

Otherwise, [an alternative outcome]. In the end, [final result].

2.1 Picnic Under Dark Clouds

A family setting up a picnic while dark clouds gather above them. What will happen next?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a family is spreading a blanket on the grass and unpacking sandwiches, but the sky is full of dark clouds.

I think that it will start raining soon. As a result, the family will rush to pack everything, and the children will probably run to the car laughing. After that, they might wait in the car and eat their lunch there, or drive home and have a picnic on the living-room floor.

Otherwise, the clouds may drift away, and they’ll be lucky. In the end, I think they’ll make the best of the day and remember the spontaneous change of plans as a funny memory.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
dark clouds grey clouds that bring rain Dark clouds covered the sky.
drift away move slowly off The storm drifted away.
spontaneous done without planning It was a spontaneous trip.

2.2 Soccer and Lightning

A soccer game in progress as lightning flashes in the distance. What do you predict?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, two teams are playing soccer on a field, and in the distance, there’s lightning in a very dark sky. The spectators are looking nervous.

I think that the referee will stop the game. As a result, the players and spectators will run for shelter in the clubhouse or their cars. After that, they’ll wait for the storm to pass, and the officials will decide whether to resume the match.

Otherwise, if the storm gets worse, they’ll postpone the game to another day. In the end, I believe safety will come first, and everyone will go home disappointed but safe.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
lightning a flash of light in a storm Lightning struck the tree.
resume start again after a stop The game will resume at three.
postpone move to a later time They postponed the match.
disappointed but safe sad about the result but unhurt We were disappointed but safe.

2.3 Rain at an Outdoor Wedding

A couple preparing for an outdoor wedding as rain begins to fall. Predict what will happen.

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a couple is standing in a garden with rows of white chairs and flowers, but raindrops are starting to fall on their outfits.

I think that the guests and the planners will quickly move everything inside. As a result, the ceremony will take place in a nearby hall or under a large tent, and everyone will help carry chairs and decorations. After that, the couple will exchange vows indoors, and the rain will add a romantic mood.

Otherwise, if the rain stops quickly, they could continue outside. In the end, I think the wedding will be unforgettable, because they’ll laugh about the backup plan for years.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
outfits sets of clothes Their outfits were soaked.
exchange vows make wedding promises They exchanged vows in the garden.
unforgettable impossible to forget It was an unforgettable day.
backup plan a second plan in case the first fails We had a backup plan for rain.

2.4 Hikers and a Storm

A group of hikers reaching a fork in the trail with a storm approaching. What will they do?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, four hikers with backpacks have stopped at a fork in the trail, and one is studying a map. Behind them, black clouds are moving in fast.

I think that they will choose the shorter path back to the parking lot. As a result, they’ll walk quickly and avoid exposed hilltops, where lightning is dangerous. After that, they’ll put on their raincoats and stay close together.

Otherwise, if the storm hits before they reach safety, they’ll look for a shelter among the trees. In the end, I think they’ll return safely, tired and wet, and they’ll plan the next trip with a better weather forecast.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
fork a place where a path divides We reached a fork in the road.
shelter a place that protects from weather We found shelter under a tree.
weather forecast a prediction of the weather Check the weather forecast first.

2.5 Melting Snowman

Children building a snowman as the sun comes out and the temperature rises. What will happen?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, two children are proudly adding a carrot nose to their snowman, and the sun has just come out from behind the clouds. The snow already looks wet and shiny.

I think that the snowman will begin to melt. As a result, its head will probably slide off, and the scarf will fall to the ground. After that, the children will feel a little sad, but they’ll probably laugh and start playing in the slush instead.

Otherwise, if the sun hides again, the snowman will survive until the evening. In the end, I think the kids will rebuild it on a colder day, and they’ll take a photo to preserve the memory.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
survive continue to exist The plant survived the frost.
rebuild build again They rebuilt the snow fort.
preserve keep safe A photo preserves a memory.

3.1 Laptop Not Connecting

A woman standing before a projector screen at a meeting, with her laptop not connecting. What will happen next?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a woman is standing in a meeting room, and the projector screen is blank. She’s pressing keys on her laptop and looks stressed, while her colleagues wait.

I think that she will try a few quick solutions, such as checking the cable or restarting the laptop. As a result, someone from the technical team may come to help. After that, the screen will probably light up, and she’ll start the presentation a few minutes late.

Otherwise, if the problem continues, she’ll improvise by sharing the slides by email or speaking without them. In the end, I think she’ll stay calm, and the team will appreciate her professionalism.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
stressed feeling pressure and worry He felt stressed before the talk.
improvise do something without preparation We improvised a plan.
appreciate value and be thankful for I appreciate your patience.

3.2 Exam Day

Students waiting outside a classroom as a teacher arrives holding exam papers. Predict what will happen.

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a group of students are standing in a hallway, and some of them are looking at their notes. A teacher is walking toward them with a stack of exam papers.

I think that the teacher will open the door and ask everyone to find a seat. As a result, the students will go in quietly, and they’ll put their bags at the front. After that, the teacher will hand out the papers and explain the rules, and the exam will start.

Otherwise, if somebody forgot a pen, they might panic. In the end, I think most students will do their best, and after the exam, they’ll feel relieved.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
exam papers printed tests The exam papers were on the desk.
do their best try as hard as they can I did my best on the test.
relieved happy because stress is over She felt relieved after the test.

3.3 Long Checkout Line

A very long line at a store checkout and one open cashier, with a manager approaching. What do you think will happen?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, there’s a long line of customers at the checkout, and only one cashier is working. Many of them look impatient, and a manager is walking over.

I think that the manager will open another register. As a result, the line will split into two, and customers will move faster. After that, the manager will probably apologize for the wait and thank everyone for their patience.

Otherwise, if no staff are available, some customers might leave their baskets and go to another store. In the end, I think the store will learn to schedule more staff during peak hours to improve the service.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
impatient annoyed at having to wait The impatient customer sighed.
peak hours the busiest times Avoid shopping at peak hours.
improve make better The store wants to improve service.

3.4 Concert Starting

A group of people waiting for a concert to begin as the lights dim and the band walks on stage. Predict what comes next.

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a big crowd is standing in a dark hall, and some are holding up their phones. The lights are dimming, and the band is walking onto the stage.

I think that the audience will start cheering loudly as soon as the first song begins. As a result, the whole place will fill with energy, and many people will sing along. After that, the band will probably play their most famous hits, and the fans will dance and wave their arms.

Otherwise, if there’s a technical problem with the sound, the show may be delayed. In the end, I think it’ll be an unforgettable night, and everyone will leave with ringing ears and big smiles.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
unforgettable impossible to forget It was an unforgettable concert.
ringing ears a buzzing sound in the ears after loud noise I left with ringing ears.
sing along to sing together with a song that is playing Everyone started to sing along.

3.5 New Employee’s First Day

A new employee arriving at an office on the first day, where colleagues are gathered with a cake. What will happen?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a young man with a backpack is walking into an office, and a group of colleagues is standing near a table with a cake and a sign that says “Welcome.”

I think that the colleagues will greet him warmly and surprise him with a small party. As a result, he’ll feel less nervous and more welcome. After that, they’ll introduce themselves, and they’ll probably show him his desk and the facilities.

Otherwise, if he’s very shy, he may be a little overwhelmed at first. In the end, I think he’ll fit in quickly and make good friends, and this gesture will make him love his new job.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
facilities rooms and equipment for use The office has great facilities.
fit in feel like part of a group He fit in well.
gesture an action that shows feeling It was a kind gesture.

4.1 Pot Boiling Over

A man on the phone while a pot boils over on the stove behind him. What will happen next?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a man is standing in the kitchen with his back to the stove, talking on the phone, and a pot behind him is boiling over. He hasn’t noticed it.

I think that the water will keep spilling onto the stove. As a result, it’ll make a mess and might even put out the flame, which could be dangerous if it’s a gas stove. After that, he’ll smell something or hear the hissing and turn around in a panic.

Otherwise, a family member may enter the kitchen and turn the heat down. In the end, I think he’ll end the call, clean up, and learn not to leave the stove unattended again.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
boiling over overflowing because of heat The milk is boiling over.
unattended left without anyone watching Don’t leave food unattended.
stove a kitchen appliance used for cooking Don’t leave the pot on the stove.

4.2 Ladder and Paint Can

A woman on a ladder painting a ceiling with an open paint can on the top step. Predict what will happen.

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a woman is standing on a tall ladder and painting the ceiling, and there’s an open can of paint on the top step, right next to her foot. The ladder looks a bit unstable.

I think that she will accidentally knock the can over with her elbow. As a result, paint will fall on the floor, the furniture, and maybe on her head. After that, she’ll climb down carefully, feel annoyed, and start cleaning up.

Otherwise, if she’s careful, she’ll move the can to a safer spot. In the end, I think she’ll finish the job, but next time she’ll use a paint tray and put a drop cloth on the floor.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
unstable not steady The ladder is unstable.
paint tray a flat container for paint Pour paint into the paint tray.
drop cloth a sheet to protect the floor Cover the floor with a drop cloth.

4.3 Moving Boxes and a Dog

I think that someone will trip over the dog. As a result, the boxes will fall and might break, and one of them could get hurt. After that, the family will stop, check for injuries, and shout at the dog in surprise.

Otherwise, if they notice the dog in time, they’ll call it to stay behind. In the end, I believe they’ll carry on more carefully, and the dog will be kept in a separate room until the move is complete.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence

separate room a different room Put the cat in a separate room.
complete finished The work is complete.

4.4 Locked Out in the Rain

A person standing outside their front door in the rain, searching their pockets, with keys visible on a table inside. What will happen?

Sample

Based on what I see in the picture, a person is standing outside a front door in heavy rain and checking every pocket. Through the window, I can see a set of keys lying on a table inside, so they’re locked out.

I think that they’ll try the door and the windows first, hoping one is open. As a result, they’ll get soaked and more frustrated. After that, they’ll probably call a family member who has a spare key, or a locksmith.

Otherwise, a neighbour may notice and offer shelter. In the end, I think they’ll get back inside after an hour, and they’ll hide a spare key in a safe place to avoid the problem in the future.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence

safe place a secure spot Keep the key in a safe place.

Vocabulary

6.2 Lost Child in a Mall
