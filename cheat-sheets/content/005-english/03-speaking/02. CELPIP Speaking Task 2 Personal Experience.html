CELPIP Speaking Task 2: Personal Experience

1 Memorable Events and Moments

Topics: a special occasion, a celebration, a surprising event, a proud moment, a day you will never forget.

General Template

Let me tell you about [the event]. [It happened when / where / with whom].

At first, [what happened]. Then, [the main moment, with details and feelings].

In the end, [the result].

Looking back, [why it was special or what you learned].

1.1 Memorable Birthday

Talk about a memorable birthday or celebration you have had. What happened and why was it special?

Sample

Let me tell you about my twenty-fifth birthday. It was a day I’ll never forget. I thought my friends had forgotten it, since nobody called me all morning.

At first, I felt a bit sad. However, in the evening my sister asked me to join her for dinner at a small restaurant. When I walked in, the lights came on, and about thirty friends and relatives yelled “Surprise!” There were balloons, a huge cake, and a banner with my name on it.

In the end, we danced and laughed until midnight, and I was overjoyed.

Looking back, it was special because so many people had worked together just to make me feel valued. I’ll treasure that memory forever.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
banner a long sign with words on it They hung a banner above the door.
overjoyed extremely happy She was overjoyed to see her family.
make me feel valued make me feel important Their kind words made me feel valued.
treasure to keep and care about deeply I treasure the photos from that day.

1.2 Feeling Proud

Describe a time when you felt very proud of yourself.

Sample

Let me tell you about the day I finished my first half marathon. A year earlier, I could hardly run for ten minutes, so I decided to challenge myself.

At first, training was exhausting. I woke up at six every morning, and my legs were sore. Then, after a few months, I noticed I was getting stronger. On race day, the weather was cold, and I felt nervous at the start line. Around kilometre eighteen, I wanted to quit, but the crowd was cheering loudly, so I kept going.

In the end, I crossed the finish line in two hours and ten minutes, and tears came to my eyes.

Looking back, I felt a deep sense of accomplishment. It taught me that perseverance can take me further than I expect.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
challenge myself test my own abilities I like to challenge myself with new goals.
cheering shouting to encourage someone The fans were cheering for the runners.
accomplishment something achieved with effort Graduating was a great accomplishment.
perseverance continuing despite difficulty Perseverance helped her pass the exam.

1.3 A Surprise

Talk about a surprise you received. What was it and how did you feel?

Sample

Let me tell you about a surprise I got last spring. I was working late at the office, feeling tired and a little stressed.

At first, I thought it was a normal evening. Then, my manager walked in with a large envelope and said it was for me. Inside, I found a plane ticket to Vancouver and a note from my husband. He had secretly arranged a weekend trip, and my boss had helped by clearing my schedule.

I was completely speechless. My heart was racing, and I couldn’t stop smiling.

In the end, we had a fantastic weekend by the ocean.

Looking back, I was so touched by his effort. It showed me how much he cares about me.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
clearing my schedule removing my other plans to free time He helped by clearing my schedule for Friday.
speechless unable to speak because of strong feelings I was speechless when I saw the gift.
touched emotionally moved She was touched by the kind message.

1.4 Best Day of Your Life

Describe the best day of your life so far.

Sample

Let me tell you about my wedding day. It took place in June, in a small garden near my hometown, and the weather was perfect.

At first, I was extremely nervous, and my hands were shaking while I was getting ready. Then, I saw my future wife walking down the aisle in a beautiful white dress, and all my worries disappeared. We exchanged vows in front of our families, and many people had tears of joy.

After that, we enjoyed a delicious dinner, heartfelt speeches, and dancing under the stars. It was a truly joyful atmosphere.

Looking back, it was the best day of my life because I was surrounded by everyone I love. It was the start of a new chapter, and I’ll cherish every moment.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
exchanged vows made promises to each other in a wedding They exchanged vows by the lake.
joyful full of happiness The joyful crowd clapped and sang.
start of a new chapter the beginning of a new stage in life Moving abroad was the start of a new chapter.
cherish to value highly I cherish my childhood friends.

1.5 Feeling Nervous

Talk about a time when you were very nervous before an event.

Sample

Let me tell you about the time I gave a speech at my best friend’s wedding. I’d never spoken in front of so many people, and I was terrified.

At first, I practised for days, but when I stood up, my mind went blank. My palms were sweaty, and my voice was trembling. I could see over a hundred faces looking at me.

Then, I took a deep breath, looked at my friend, and started with a funny story about our university days. Everyone laughed, and I relaxed immediately.

In the end, the speech went well, and people thanked me afterwards.

Looking back, I learned that being nervous is normal, and a genuine story is better than a perfect speech. I felt a great sense of relief.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
trembling shaking slightly His hands were trembling with fear.
genuine honest and real She gave a genuine smile.
sense of relief the feeling that a worry has gone I felt a sense of relief when it ended.

1.6 Family Event at Home ⭐

Talk about a memorable event you had with your family at home. What was it, when was it, who was there, what happened, and why was it memorable?

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 4.

Sample

Let me tell you about my parents’ thirtieth anniversary, which we celebrated at home two years ago. My sisters and I wanted to pull off a surprise, so we kept it a secret for weeks.

At first, we cleaned the house and put up lights and photos from every decade. Then, we invited all the relatives and told them to hide in the living room. When my parents walked in, everyone shouted “Congratulations!”, and my mother burst into tears. There was a huge cake, and my uncle gave a heartfelt speech.

In the end, we danced and shared stories until midnight, and the whole family bonded over old memories.

Looking back, it was special because it brought three generations together under one roof, and I will treasure it forever.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
pull off succeed in doing something difficult They pulled off a perfect surprise.
put up hang or place on a wall We put up balloons.
bond over become closer by sharing something We bonded over our love of music.
heartfelt sincere and deeply felt She gave a heartfelt thank-you.
treasure keep and care about deeply I treasure those photos.
anniversary a yearly celebration of a past event They celebrated their anniversary.

1.7 Exciting Memory ⭐

Tell about an exciting memory in your life.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 4.

Sample

Let me tell you about the day I went skydiving for the first time. I had always been afraid of heights, but I wanted to push myself out of my comfort zone, so I signed up with a friend.

At first, my heart was racing as we boarded the small plane. Then, when the door opened, I took a deep breath and jumped. The first few seconds were terrifying, but suddenly I felt free. I could see the whole city below me, and I let out a loud scream of joy.

In the end, we landed safely in a green field, and I could not stop smiling.

Looking back, it was the most thrilling moment of my life, and it taught me to face up to my fears.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
take a deep breath breathe in slowly to calm down I took a deep breath before the speech.
let out make a sound He let out a sigh of relief.
face up to accept and deal with She faced up to her mistake.
push myself out of my comfort zone try something difficult and new Travelling alone pushed me out of my comfort zone.
board get on a plane, train or ship Passengers board at gate five.
thrilling very exciting The ride was thrilling.

Topics: a holiday, a memorable trip, a place you visited, a getaway, getting lost.

General Template

2.1 Memorable Trip ⭐

Talk about a memorable trip you have taken. Where did you go and what did you do?

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 3 (a long question with several examples, such as a memorable trip, a special experience or a day with family).

Sample

Let me tell you about my trip to Banff National Park two summers ago. I went with three close friends, and we drove from Calgary.

At first, I was amazed by the breathtaking mountains and the turquoise lakes. Then, we spent three days hiking, canoeing, and camping under the stars. One morning, we even saw a bear from a safe distance, which was both exciting and scary.

After that, we relaxed in the hot springs, and our tired muscles felt wonderful.

Looking back, it was memorable because I was totally disconnected from the city life. I came home feeling refreshed, and I’m already dreaming of going back.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
breathtaking extremely beautiful The view from the top was breathtaking.
disconnected from separated from I felt disconnected from my daily stress.
refreshed full of energy again I woke up refreshed after the trip.
hot springs natural pools of warm water We soaked in the hot springs.

2.2 A Place to Revisit

Describe a place that you visited and would like to go back to.

Sample

Let me tell you about Lisbon, the capital of Portugal. I spent a week there last autumn, and I fell in love with it.

At first, I loved the colourful buildings and the narrow cobblestone streets. Then, I discovered the local food. I tried fresh grilled sardines and the famous custard tarts, which were absolutely delicious. In the evenings, I listened to live music in small bars, and the people were incredibly welcoming.

In the end, I didn’t want to leave.

Looking back, I’d love to return because I only saw a small part of the city. I’d like to explore the nearby beaches and soak up the laid-back atmosphere again.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
welcoming friendly to guests The hosts were warm and welcoming.
soak up enjoy and absorb I soaked up the sunshine on the beach.
laid-back relaxed and easygoing It’s a laid-back town with slow mornings.

Looking back, it was memorable because it was so peaceful. I felt completely recharged afterward.

Looking back, it turned out to be a blessing in disguise. I learned to stay calm and make the best of unexpected situations.

2.7 Unexpected Weather ⭐

Talk about a time when the weather changed unexpectedly or affected your plans. Where and when was it, who were you with, and how did it affect you?

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 11, Oct 9 and in the Oct 2 compiled list.

Sample

Let me tell you about a picnic I planned with my friends last June. The forecast said it would be sunny all day, so we headed out to the lake with sandwiches and a speaker.

At first, everything was perfect. Then, around two o’clock, dark clouds rolled in and a sudden downpour began. We grabbed our things and ran to a small shelter, laughing the whole time. Our clothes were soaked, and the food was ruined.

However, we decided to make the best of it. We shared the dry snacks, played cards and watched the storm over the lake.

In the end, it became one of my funniest memories.

Looking back, the surprise rain made the day more unforgettable than a perfect one would have been.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
head out to leave for a place We headed out to the beach early.
roll in arrive slowly, like clouds or fog Fog rolled in over the harbour.
make the best of do as well as possible in a bad situation Let’s make the best of the delay.
downpour a heavy fall of rain A downpour flooded the street.
soaked completely wet I got soaked on the way home.
forecast a prediction of the weather The forecast says it will snow.

2.8 Language Barrier ⭐

In the end, I left the shop feeling grateful.

Looking back, I learned that kindness is a universal language.

2.14 Unusual Transportation ⭐

Recent exam, October 2026: reported in the Oct 2 compiled list.

Sample

Let me tell you about the time I rode a tuk-tuk in Thailand. I had never seen one before, and I wanted to try out something different on my vacation.

At first, I was a bit nervous, because the vehicle was small and open on the sides. Then, the driver weaved through the busy traffic, and I held on tightly. The wind in my face and the colourful street signs made it a real adventure. I also bargained with the driver and got a fair price.
