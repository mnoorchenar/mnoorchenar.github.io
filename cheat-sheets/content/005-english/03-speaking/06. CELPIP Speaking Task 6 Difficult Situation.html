CELPIP Speaking Task 6: Difficult Situation

1 Neighbours and Housing Problems

Topics: noise, parking, pets, maintenance, shared spaces, a landlord’s rules.

General Template

Hi, I hope I’m not disturbing you. I wanted to talk to you about [the problem].

The thing is, [explain the problem and how it affects you]. I understand that [show empathy].

That’s why [your polite request or solution]. In that case, [the benefit for both sides].

Thank you so much for your understanding.

1.1 Loud Music

Your neighbour plays loud music late at night. Talk to them politely and ask them to stop.

Sample

Hi, I hope I’m not disturbing you. I wanted to talk to you about the music at night.

The thing is, I’ve been hearing it until around two in the morning for the past few days, and I have to get up early for work. It’s been hard to get enough sleep. I understand that you enjoy music, and I’m not against it at all.

That’s why I was wondering if you could turn the volume down after ten o’clock, or use headphones. In that case, we’d both be happy, and I wouldn’t have to file a complaint with the building manager, which I’d rather avoid.

Thank you so much for your understanding. I really appreciate it.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
file a complaint make an official report about a problem I filed a complaint about the noise.
appreciate be thankful for I appreciate your help.
volume how loud a sound is Please keep the volume low at night.

1.2 Barking Dog

Your neighbour’s dog barks all day. Talk to your neighbour about the problem and suggest a solution.

Sample

Hi, I hope I’m not disturbing you. I wanted to talk to you about your dog, Max.

The thing is, he barks for hours while you’re at work, and I work from home, so it’s hard for me to concentrate. I understand that he’s probably lonely or bored, and I love dogs, so I don’t blame him.

That’s why I’d like to suggest a few ideas. You could leave him with a chew toy, or hire a dog walker to take him out at lunchtime. In that case, he’d be tired and calm, and the barking would decrease a lot. I’d be happy to help you find a reliable walker.

Thank you so much for your understanding. I’m sure we can work this out.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
decrease become less Noise will decrease.
reliable dependable A reliable service.
work this out solve a problem together We’ll work this out.

1.3 Parked in Your Space

A neighbour often parks in your space. Speak to them and ask them to stop.

Sample

Hi, I hope I’m not disturbing you. I wanted to talk to you about the parking space in front of building B.

The thing is, the spot with my apartment number on it has been taken several times this week, and I’ve had to park far away and carry my groceries a long way. I understand that you might have made a mistake, or visitors may have used it.

That’s why I’d really appreciate it if you could use your own assigned space from now on. In that case, we won’t have any misunderstandings, and I won’t need to ask the management to tow any vehicles, which I’d prefer not to do.

Thank you so much for your understanding. Have a great day!

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
assigned given to a particular person An assigned seat.
misunderstandings confusion between people There were misunderstandings.
tow pull a vehicle away They towed my car.

1.4 Broken Heater

Your landlord has not fixed a broken heater for weeks. Call them and ask for urgent repairs.

Sample

Hello, I hope I’m not disturbing you. I’m calling about the heater in my apartment.

The thing is, it stopped working three weeks ago, and I’ve already left two messages. The temperature is dropping below zero at night, and my family has been using space heaters, which isn’t safe. I understand that you’re busy, and I know repairs can take time.

That’s why I’m asking you to send a technician within the next two days. In that case, the problem will be solved, and I won’t have to escalate the issue to the housing authority, since a landlord is legally responsible for heating.

Thank you so much for your understanding. Please call me back with a time.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
space heaters small portable heating devices Space heaters can be a fire risk.
escalate take a problem to a higher level I had to escalate the complaint.
legally responsible required by law to do something The owner is legally responsible.

1.5 Dirty Kitchen

Your roommate leaves the kitchen dirty. Talk to them and suggest a cleaning schedule.

Sample

Hi, I hope I’m not disturbing you. I wanted to talk to you about the kitchen.

The thing is, there have been dirty dishes in the sink for several days, and it’s starting to smell. I feel a little uncomfortable cooking there. I understand that you’ve been very busy with exams, and I’m not blaming you.

That’s why I’d like to suggest a simple cleaning schedule. We could take turns each week, so everybody has clear responsibilities. In that case, the kitchen would stay tidy, and we’d avoid any tension.

Thank you so much for your understanding. If it’s easier, we can make a chart together this evening.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
uncomfortable not at ease I feel uncomfortable.
cleaning schedule a plan for who cleans and when We made a cleaning schedule.
clear responsibilities well-defined duties Everyone has clear responsibilities.

1.6 Storing a Friend’s Things ⭐

Your friend Sara wants to bring some of her things from storage to your home. Your housemate Samantha objects because the unit is small and the landlord wants the home kept tidy. Speak to one of them, explain the situation and suggest a solution.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 4.

Sample

Hi, I hope I’m not disturbing you, Sara. I wanted to talk to you about your boxes from storage.

The thing is, our apartment is quite small, and Samantha is worried that the clutter will get out of hand. On top of that, our landlord wants us to keep the place tidy, and he drops by for inspections. I understand that paying for storage is expensive and that you’re on a tight budget.

That’s why I’d like to suggest a compromise. You could bring only the essential items, and we can clear out a corner of the closet for them. In that case, you’ll save money, and we’ll stay on good terms with the landlord and Samantha.

Thank you so much for your understanding. I’m sure we can work something out.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
get out of hand become impossible to control The mess got out of hand.
drop by visit briefly The landlord dropped by yesterday.
clear out empty and clean We cleared out the garage.
work out find a solution Let’s work out a plan.
on a tight budget with very little money to spend She’s on a tight budget this month.
stay on good terms keep a friendly relationship We stay on good terms with our neighbours.
clutter untidy things lying around The clutter filled the hallway.

2 Workplace Situations

Topics: a coworker issue, a deadline, asking for time off, a change in schedule, shared tasks, a boss.

General Template

Hi, do you have a minute? I wanted to talk to you about [the problem].

The thing is, [explain the problem and how it affects the team]. I understand that [show empathy].

That’s why [your polite request or solution]. In that case, [the benefit for both sides].

Thank you so much for your understanding.

2.1 Late Coworker

A coworker often arrives late to team meetings. Talk to them and ask them to be on time.

Sample

Hi, do you have a minute? I wanted to talk to you about our team meetings.

The thing is, you’ve arrived about ten minutes late for the last few meetings, and we’ve had to repeat information. It slows everybody down. I understand that you have a lot on your plate, and your schedule is busy.

That’s why I’d like to ask you to join on time, or let us know if something comes up. In that case, we could rearrange the order of topics, and everyone would stay on the same page. If the time doesn’t suit you, we could also move the meeting to the afternoon.

Thank you so much for your understanding. I value your work, and I just want us to run smoothly.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
rearrange change the order or plan Let’s rearrange the agenda.
on the same page sharing the same understanding We’re on the same page.
value think highly of I value your help.

2.2 Absent Colleague ⭐

You are the owner of a company, or a colleague, and one employee is constantly absent or late, often to look after family, so another employee has to cover their duties. Persuade one of them or work out a solution.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 11 and Oct 10.

Sample

Hi, do you have a minute? I wanted to talk to you about your recent schedule.

The thing is, you’ve been absent or late several times this month, and your teammate has had to cover for you. It’s putting extra pressure on her, and our deadlines are starting to slip. I understand that you’re taking care of your family, and I truly respect that.

That’s why I’d like us to find a better arrangement together. For example, you could work flexible hours or make up for the lost time on weekends. In that case, your colleague won’t be overloaded, and you’ll keep your job and your peace of mind.

Thank you so much for your understanding. Let’s touch base next week to see how it’s going.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
cover for do someone’s work while they are absent I covered for him on Monday.
take care of look after She takes care of her parents.
make up for compensate for I’ll make up for the lost hours.
slip fall behind The schedule is slipping.
overloaded given too much work The team is overloaded.
touch base briefly make contact to share news Let’s touch base on Friday.

2.3 Day Off Request

You need to ask your manager for a day off at a busy time. Explain your reason and offer a solution.

Sample

Hi, do you have a minute? I wanted to talk to you about taking a day off next Friday.

The thing is, I know it’s a busy period, with the project deadline coming up. However, my sister is getting married that day, and I really can’t miss it. I understand that the timing isn’t ideal.

That’s why I’d like to propose a solution. I’ll finish my main tasks by Thursday, and I’ll hand over the rest to a colleague with clear notes. In that case, the team will have no disruption, and I’ll be available by phone for any urgent question.

Thank you so much for your understanding. I’ll make sure everything is covered.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
hand over give responsibility to someone else I handed over the files.
disruption a break in normal activity There was no disruption.
covered managed by someone My shift is covered.

2.4 Teammate Not Helping

A teammate is not doing their share of a group project. Talk to them about it.

Sample

Hi, do you have a minute? I wanted to talk to you about our group project.

The thing is, I’ve noticed that several of your tasks are still unfinished, and the deadline is next week. I’m worried that we won’t be able to deliver on time. I understand that you may be overloaded with other work.

That’s why I’d like us to review the task list together and see what you can realistically handle. If it’s too much, we can redistribute some parts. In that case, everybody will feel the work is fair, and we’ll finish with a better result.

Thank you so much for your understanding. I really want us to succeed as a team.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
overloaded given too much work I’m overloaded this week.
redistribute share out again Let’s redistribute the tasks.
as a team together We’ll succeed as a team.

2.5 Whose Name Comes First ⭐

You finished a team project with classmates or colleagues. One friend wrote most of it and says their name should be first on the cover; another says most of the ideas were theirs and their name should be first. Persuade one of them.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 11 (two reports).

Sample

Hi, do you have a minute? I wanted to talk to you about the cover page of our project.

The thing is, I know you wrote most of the report, and that took a lot of time. I understand that you want your efforts to be recognized. However, Alex came up with the main ideas, and without them, the project would fall flat.

That’s why I think we should list the names in alphabetical order, or write “equal contribution” under all of them. In that case, nobody will feel overlooked, and the teacher will see that we pulled together as a team. You could also take the lead in the presentation, since you know the text best.

Thank you so much for your understanding. I believe this is a fair solution.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
fall flat fail to have the intended effect The joke fell flat.
pull together work as a team The staff pulled together.
take the lead be in charge She took the lead in the meeting.
overlooked not given attention His work was overlooked.
fair just and equal That’s a fair deal.
equal contribution the same amount of work from each person The paper notes equal contribution.

2.6 Mistake in Report

You made a mistake in a report your boss will present tomorrow. Tell your boss and explain your plan.

Sample

Hi, do you have a minute? I wanted to talk to you about the sales report for tomorrow’s presentation.

The thing is, I found an error in the figures for the third quarter. I entered the wrong number, so the total is about five percent too high. I’m very sorry, and I take full responsibility. I understand that this is inconvenient so close to the meeting.

That’s why I’ve already prepared a corrected version, and I’ll double-check every figure tonight. In that case, you’ll have the accurate report first thing in the morning, and I’ll explain the correction if anyone asks.

Thank you so much for your understanding. I’ll make sure it doesn’t happen again.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
take full responsibility accept blame completely I take full responsibility.
inconvenient causing trouble An inconvenient time.
correction a change that fixes an error A quick correction.

2.7 Borrowed Equipment

Your coworker keeps borrowing your equipment without asking. Speak to them politely.

Sample

Hi, do you have a minute? I wanted to talk to you about my stapler and headphones.

The thing is, I’ve noticed that they’ve disappeared from my desk a few times, and I only found them later at your desk. It’s a little frustrating, because I sometimes need them urgently. I understand that you probably didn’t mean any harm, and you may have thought I wouldn’t mind.

That’s why I’d appreciate it if you could ask me first. In that case, I’d be happy to lend them, and we’d avoid any awkwardness. Also, if you need your own set, I can recommend where to order one.

Thank you so much for your understanding. I just want to keep a good working relationship.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
awkwardness an uncomfortable feeling There was some awkwardness.
recommend suggest as good I recommend this store.
a good working relationship friendly cooperation at work We have a good working relationship.

3 Customer Service and Purchases

Topics: a faulty product, a wrong order, a bad meal, a refund, a delayed delivery, a hotel problem.

General Template

Hello, I’m calling about [the problem]. [I bought / ordered it on date].

The issue is [explain the problem]. I understand that [show empathy].

That’s why [I would like a refund / replacement / correction]. In that case, [the benefit for both sides].

Thank you so much for your help.

3.1 Faulty Phone

You bought a phone that stopped working after two days. Call the store and ask for a replacement or refund.

Sample

Hello, I’m calling about a phone that I bought from your store on Monday.

The issue is, it stopped working after two days. The screen went black, and it won’t turn on, even after I charged it overnight. I have the receipt and the original box. I understand that these things can happen, even with a good product.

That’s why I’d like a replacement, or a full refund if you don’t have the same model in stock. In that case, I can come to the store tomorrow, and I’ll bring everything with me, including the warranty card.

Thank you so much for your help. I’d appreciate a quick solution.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
charged filled with electric power I charged the phone.
replacement a new item instead of a faulty one They sent a replacement.
warranty a promise to repair or replace The warranty lasts a year.

3.2 Wrong Restaurant Order

Your restaurant order arrived wrong and cold. Talk to the server and ask for a solution.

Sample

Excuse me, I’m sorry to bother you, but I’d like to talk about my order.

The issue is, I ordered the grilled chicken, but I received the fish, and it’s also lukewarm. I’m sure it was a simple mix-up. I understand that the restaurant is very busy tonight.

That’s why I’d like you to bring me the chicken, and please make sure that it’s hot. In that case, I’ll happily wait a few extra minutes. If that’s not possible, I’d be glad to have the dish removed from the bill.

Thank you so much for your help. I really appreciate your quick attention.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
lukewarm slightly warm The soup was lukewarm.
removed taken away The item was removed.
appreciate be thankful for I appreciate it.

3.3 Automatic 20% Tip ⭐

You are at a restaurant with a friend or colleagues. The server automatically added a 20% tip to the bill. Ask for a new bill without the tip so you can decide, or accept it, and persuade your friend.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 11 (two reports).

Sample

Excuse me, I’m sorry to bother you, but I wanted to talk to you about the bill.

The thing is, a 20% tip has been added automatically, and nobody told us about it. I understand that the service was good and that servers rely on tips for their income. We would be happy to leave a generous tip.

That’s why I’d like to ask for a new bill without the tip, so that we can make our own decision. In that case, we’ll pay the tip separately, and everything will be clear and fair. If you could sort this out, we would really appreciate it.

Thank you so much for your understanding. We enjoyed the meal, and we’ll stand by our promise to tip well.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
rely on depend on Many servers rely on tips.
sort out solve a problem Please sort this out.
stand by keep a promise or support I stand by my words.
leave a generous tip give an extra amount of money for good service We left a generous tip.
make our own decision choose by ourselves We want to make our own decision.
automatically without being asked The fee was added automatically.

3.4 Noisy Hotel Room

Your hotel room is noisy and dirty. Talk to the front desk and ask for a different room.

Sample

Hello, I’m calling from room 412, and I’d like to talk about the condition of my room.

The issue is, the room is quite dirty. The bathroom hasn’t been cleaned, and there are stains on the sheets. In addition, it’s very noisy, because it faces the street, and I can hear the traffic all night. I understand that the hotel is fully booked, and housekeeping is busy.

That’s why I’d like to be moved to a quieter and cleaner room, preferably on a higher floor. In that case, I can pack my things right away, and I’m happy to wait in the lobby. I’d also like a discount for the inconvenience.

Thank you so much for your help. I’m sure you can sort this out.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
discount a reduction in price We got a discount.
sort this out solve a problem Please sort this out.
housekeeping the staff who clean hotel rooms Housekeeping changes the sheets daily.

3.5 Late Package

Your package is two weeks late. Call customer service to find out what happened.

Sample

Hello, I’m calling about an order that I placed on the fifth of March.

The issue is, it was supposed to arrive within five days, but two weeks have passed, and I still haven’t received it. The tracking number shows no movement since last Tuesday. I understand that delays happen, especially in busy periods.

That’s why I’d like to know where the package is, and when I can expect it. In that case, I can plan my week. If it’s lost, I’d prefer you reship the item by express delivery at no extra charge, or refund my money.

Thank you so much for your help. Could you please send me an email to confirm this?

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
tracking number a code for following a parcel Enter the tracking number.
reship send again They reshipped my order.
express delivery faster shipping for an extra fee I paid extra for express delivery.

3.6 Extra Bill Charge

You were charged extra on a bill. Call the company and ask for a correction.

Sample

Hello, I’m calling about my latest phone bill.

The issue is, I was charged an extra thirty dollars labelled “service fee,” and I never agreed to it. My plan costs fifty dollars a month, but the total is eighty. I understand that mistakes can occur in a large system.

That’s why I’d like you to review my account and remove the extra charge. In that case, I’ll pay the correct amount right away. I’d also like to receive a corrected bill by email, and an explanation of how it happened.

Thank you so much for your help. I’ve been a loyal customer for years, and I’d like to keep it that way.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
corrected fixed to be accurate A corrected invoice.
loyal customer someone who keeps buying from a company A loyal customer for ten years.
keep it that way continue a situation I want to keep it that way.

4 Friends, Family, and Social Conflicts

Topics: a broken promise, a borrowed item, cancelling plans, a disagreement, an awkward request.

General Template

Hey, can we talk for a minute? I wanted to talk to you about [the problem].

The thing is, [explain how you feel and why]. I understand that [show empathy].

That’s why [your polite request or solution]. In that case, [the benefit for both sides].

Thanks for hearing me out.

4.1 Damaged Car

Your friend borrowed your car and returned it with damage. Talk to your friend about it.

Sample

Hey, can we talk for a minute? I wanted to talk to you about my car.

The thing is, when you returned it yesterday, I noticed a dent on the back door and a scratch on the bumper. I didn’t want to make a big deal, but I’m a bit upset, because I take great care of that car. I understand that accidents happen, and I’m sure it wasn’t on purpose.

That’s why I’d like us to get an estimate from a repair shop and decide how to cover the cost. In that case, we can solve this fairly, and our friendship will stay intact. Next time, I’d also appreciate it if you told me right away about any damage.

Thanks for hearing me out.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
dent a hollow made by a hit A dent in the door.
intact not damaged The friendship stayed intact.
damage harm to something The storm caused damage.

4.2 Cancelled Plans

A friend cancelled plans at the last minute three times. Tell them how you feel.

Sample

Hey, can we talk for a minute? I wanted to talk to you about our plans.

The thing is, this is the third time you’ve cancelled at the last minute, and I’ve started to feel let down. I look forward to our meetings, and I often rearrange my schedule to make time. I understand that you’ve been busy and stressed lately.

That’s why I’d like us to be more realistic. If you’re not sure you can come, please tell me a day earlier. In that case, I won’t be disappointed, and we can reschedule to a time that suits you. Our friendship is important to me.

Thanks for hearing me out.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
let down disappointed I felt let down.
realistic sensible about what can be done Be realistic.
reschedule change the time of an event Let’s reschedule.

4.3 Uninvited Guests

Your sibling wants to bring friends to a family event you planned for just the family. Talk to them about it.

Sample

Hey, can we talk for a minute? I wanted to talk to you about Saturday’s dinner.

The thing is, I planned it as a small evening for the family only, because we rarely get to be together, and I cooked for exactly ten people. I understand that you want your friends to meet everyone, and that’s a nice thought.

That’s why I’d like to suggest another day for them. We could organize a casual barbecue next weekend, and I’d gladly help. In that case, we’d have a cozy family dinner now, and your friends would get a proper welcome later.

Thanks for hearing me out. I hope you don’t take it the wrong way.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
casual relaxed and informal A casual dinner.
cozy warm and intimate A cozy evening.
don’t take it the wrong way don’t misunderstand my meaning Please don’t take it the wrong way.

4.4 Refusing a Loan

You must tell your friend that you cannot lend them money. Explain politely.

Sample

Hey, can we talk for a minute? I wanted to talk to you about the money you asked about.

The thing is, I really wish I could help, but I can’t lend you that amount right now. I’m saving for rent and some unexpected bills, and my budget is tight. I understand that you’re in a difficult situation, and I feel bad saying no.

That’s why I’d like to help in other ways. I could help you make a repayment plan, or look for community support programs. In that case, you’d have a real solution, and our friendship wouldn’t be affected by money.

Thanks for hearing me out. I hope you understand.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
budget a spending plan My budget is tight.
repayment plan a schedule to pay back money We made a repayment plan.
community a local group Community programs help.

4.5 Unwanted Advice

A relative keeps giving you advice you do not want. Politely talk to them about it.
