CELPIP Speaking Task 7: Expressing Opinions

1 Education and Learning

Topics: school rules, online learning, homework, university costs, languages, teaching methods.

General Template

In my opinion, [your position]. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, [reason 1 with an explanation]. For example, [a short example].

Moreover, [reason 2]. On the other hand, [one opposing point], but [why it is weaker].

All in all, [restate your position]. That’s why I firmly believe that [conclusion].

1.1 School Uniforms

Should schools require students to wear uniforms? Why or why not?

Sample

In my opinion, schools should require uniforms. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, uniforms create equality. When everybody wears the same clothes, nobody feels judged for their brand names or their family’s income, so students can focus on learning. For example, in my school, there was much less peer pressure about fashion.

Moreover, uniforms save time and money. Parents don’t have to buy lots of trendy outfits, and students don’t have to waste time choosing what to wear every morning. On the other hand, some people say that uniforms limit self-expression, but students can still show their personality through hobbies, hairstyles, and accessories.

All in all, uniforms promote a sense of belonging and a more serious learning environment. That’s why I firmly believe that schools should keep them.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
brand names the names of well-known makers Teens often want brand names.
peer pressure influence from people your age Peer pressure can be strong.
self-expression showing who you are Art is a form of self-expression.
belonging feeling accepted in a group A sense of belonging.
learning environment the setting where students learn A calm learning environment.

1.2 Online Learning

Is online learning as effective as learning in a classroom? Explain your opinion.

Sample

In my opinion, online learning is not as effective as classroom learning for most students. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, face-to-face lessons offer direct interaction. Students can ask questions immediately, and teachers can see when someone is confused. For example, in my own online course, I often felt lost, and nobody noticed.

Moreover, it requires strong self-discipline. At home, there are many distractions, such as phones, family, and social media, so it’s easy to fall behind. On the other hand, online classes are flexible and cheaper, but that doesn’t mean that students learn more. They often miss the social side of school, like group work and friendships.

All in all, online learning is a useful supplement, but it can’t fully replace classrooms. That’s why I firmly believe that in-person education remains more effective.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
self-discipline the ability to control yourself Online study needs self-discipline.
group work tasks done together Group work builds teamwork.
supplement something that adds to something else A useful supplement.

1.3 Daily Homework

Should children have homework every day? Give your opinion.

Sample

In my opinion, children shouldn’t have homework every day. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, kids need time to rest and play. After spending six or seven hours at school, they’re tired, and too much homework causes stress and burnout. For example, my nephew stays up until ten every night with assignments, and he’s often exhausted in class.

Moreover, children also learn important things outside books, such as sports, creativity, and family bonding. On the other hand, homework helps students review the lesson, but a few times a week is enough, and the tasks should be short and meaningful, not just busywork.

All in all, a balanced schedule gives children better health and better motivation. That’s why I firmly believe that schools should limit daily homework.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
burnout exhaustion from too much stress Burnout affects performance.
bonding forming close relationships Family bonding matters.
busywork pointless tasks that fill time The worksheet was busywork.
motivation the desire to do something Rest improves motivation.

1.4 Free University

Should university education be free for everyone? Explain.

Sample

In my opinion, university education should be free, or at least much cheaper. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, it would give everyone equal opportunities. Talented students from poor families often can’t attend because of tuition fees, and society loses their potential. For example, in some European countries, higher education is free, and more people graduate.

Moreover, an educated population benefits the entire economy. Graduates earn more, pay more taxes, and fill skilled jobs. On the other hand, critics say it would cost the government too much, but the long-term return is greater than the cost, and students wouldn’t carry heavy student loans for decades.

All in all, free education is an investment in the future. That’s why I firmly believe that governments should make it available to all.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
tuition fees the money paid for teaching Tuition fees are rising.
skilled trained and able A skilled worker.
student loans borrowed money for study Student loans are heavy.
investment spending that brings later benefit Education is an investment.

1.5 Paying for Future Buildings ⭐

Should university students pay for the cost of buildings that will be finished after they graduate? Give your opinion and reasons.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 9 and Oct 8 (two reports).

Sample

In my opinion, students should not pay for buildings that they will never use. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, it’s simply unfair. Tuition is already very high, and students who pay through the nose deserve to benefit from what they fund. For example, a student who graduates in May will never enter a new library that opens in September.

Moreover, the cost of construction should be shared by the government, donors and future students who will reap the benefits. On the other hand, some people say that today’s graduates also enjoyed buildings paid for by others, but that does not justify asking students to foot the bill for something they won’t use.

All in all, such fees would put a strain on students’ finances. That’s why I firmly believe that universities should find other sources of funding.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
pay through the nose pay too much We paid through the nose for the tickets.
foot the bill pay for something Who will foot the bill?
put a strain on cause pressure for Debt puts a strain on families.
reap the benefits enjoy the good results Students reap the benefits of a modern campus.
funding money provided for a purpose The school needs more funding.
tuition the fee for teaching Tuition rises every year.

1.6 Financial Skills

Should schools teach financial skills as a required subject? Why or why not?

Sample

In my opinion, schools should definitely teach financial skills. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, these skills are essential for adult life. Many young people leave school without knowing how to make a budget, understand interest, or avoid debt. For example, I know people who got into serious trouble with credit cards at the age of twenty.

Moreover, learning early builds good habits, such as saving and investing. On the other hand, some say the curriculum is already full, but a short weekly class could replace outdated subjects. Parents may not always know how to teach money management, either.

All in all, financial literacy protects people from mistakes and gives them independence. That’s why I firmly believe that it should be a required subject.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
budget a plan for spending money Make a monthly budget.
investing putting money into something to earn more Investing takes patience.
financial literacy understanding how money works Financial literacy is vital.
independence freedom from control by others Money gives independence.

1.7 Phones in Class ⭐

Should mobile phones be banned in classrooms? Give your opinion.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 4 (smartphones banned for high-school students).

Sample

In my opinion, phones should be banned in classrooms. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, they are a major distraction. Students check messages and social media instead of listening, and studies show that even a phone on the desk reduces concentration. For example, in my cousin’s class, the grades improved after the school introduced a phone-free rule.

Moreover, phones can lead to cheating and cyberbullying. On the other hand, phones can be useful for research, but schools can provide tablets or computers for that, and students can use phones at lunch or in an emergency.

All in all, a ban helps students focus and interact more with each other. That’s why I firmly believe that schools should keep phones out of classrooms.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
distraction something that takes attention away Phones are a distraction.
cheating breaking the rules to get a better result Cheating is serious.
cyberbullying harassing others online Cyberbullying hurts students.

1.8 Phones at Lunchtime ⭐

Should students be allowed to use their phones at lunchtime?

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 8 (two reports) and Oct 7.

Sample

In my opinion, students should be allowed to use their phones during lunch. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, lunch is a break from lessons, and students need to relax. Using a phone allows them to wind down, listen to music or get in touch with their families. For example, a student may need to call a parent about a doctor’s appointment.

Moreover, phones are also useful for studying. Students can look up a word or review notes before the next class. On the other hand, some teachers worry that students will lose touch with each other and sit in silence, but schools can set clear rules, such as phone-free tables for people who want to chat.

All in all, controlled phone use during lunch is harmless and even beneficial. That’s why I firmly believe that schools should lift the ban for this short break.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
wind down relax after stress I wind down with music.
get in touch with contact He got in touch with his mother.
look up search for information Look up the meaning online.
lose touch with stop communicating with They lost touch with old friends.
harmless not causing damage It’s a harmless habit.
lift the ban allow something that was forbidden The city lifted the ban on skateboards.
phone-free where phones are not allowed The class is phone-free.

1.9 Extracurricular Activities

Do local schools offer enough extracurricular activities for teenagers? Explain your view.

Sample

In my opinion, local schools don’t offer enough extracurricular activities. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, there is limited variety. Most schools have a few sports teams, but few options for arts, technology, or volunteering. For example, a teenager who loves robotics or theatre may have nowhere to go after school.

Moreover, budgets are limited, so activities are often cut first. That’s a pity, because teens develop teamwork, leadership, and confidence through clubs. On the other hand, some schools do a great job, but they’re the exception, and many students can’t afford fees or transportation to private programs.

All in all, more funding would help young people stay engaged and out of trouble. That’s why I firmly believe that schools need to expand these programs.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
arts creative subjects like music and drama Arts classes were cut.
teamwork working well together Teamwork wins games.
transportation a way of travelling There’s no transportation after school.
funding money provided for a purpose The club needs funding.
engaged actively involved Engaged students do better.

1.10 Comments Instead of Grades ⭐

Do you agree that teachers should give comments instead of grades to assess students?

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 11.

Sample

In my opinion, comments are more useful than grades alone. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, written feedback explains exactly what a student did well and what to improve. A grade of seventy percent doesn’t tell a student how to make progress. For example, a comment like “Your argument is strong, but your conclusion needs more detail” gives clear direction.

Moreover, grades can create stress and unhealthy competition. Students may fixate on numbers instead of learning, and some may give up on a subject after a bad mark. On the other hand, grades are quick and easy to compare, but they don’t reflect effort or creativity.

All in all, comments encourage growth and reduce anxiety. That’s why I firmly believe that teachers should lean toward detailed feedback, while keeping grades as a simple summary.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
fixate on think about too much Don’t fixate on mistakes.
give up on stop trying with She gave up on math.
lean toward prefer I lean toward the first option.
make progress improve He is making progress in English.
reflect show Marks don’t always reflect ability.
feedback comments on how well someone did The teacher gave helpful feedback.

1.11 Teen Part-Time Jobs ⭐

Should high school students have a part-time job? Will students with part-time jobs be more successful in the future than those without?

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 4 (several reports) and in the Oct 2 reports.

Sample

In my opinion, high school students benefit from a part-time job, as long as it’s reasonable. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, a job teaches valuable life skills, such as punctuality, teamwork and time management. For example, a teenager who works at a café learns how to deal with customers politely and handle stress.

Moreover, earning their own money gives teenagers a sense of independence. They learn to budget and may even put money aside for university. On the other hand, a job can take time away from studying, but a few hours on weekends will not get in the way of good grades.

All in all, part-time work builds maturity and prepares students for the real world. That’s why I firmly believe that it is a positive experience, and it can give students a head start in their careers.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
deal with handle She deals with complaints.
put money aside save money I put money aside every month.
get in the way of prevent or block Games shouldn’t get in the way of homework.
give a head start give an early advantage Experience gives students a head start.
time management the skill of organizing your time Good time management reduces stress.
budget plan how to spend money He learned to budget.
maturity the quality of behaving like an adult Work builds maturity.

1.12 Learning a Language Abroad ⭐

Do you think people learning a foreign language should live in a country where it is spoken? Should schools send students who study a foreign language to that country for a term?

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 9 (two reports).

Sample

In my opinion, living in the country where a language is spoken is the best way to learn it. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, immersion forces you to practise every day. You must order food, ask for directions and make small talk, and that experience helps you pick up natural expressions that textbooks don’t teach. For example, my cousin learned more French in three months in Montreal than in two years at school.

Moreover, you also learn the culture, humour and customs, which makes you a more fluent speaker. On the other hand, living abroad is expensive, but schools can offer scholarships and short exchange programs to make it affordable.

All in all, studying abroad speeds up learning and builds confidence. That’s why I firmly believe that schools should encourage students to spend a term in the country.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
make small talk chat about light topics We made small talk in the elevator.
pick up learn casually She picked up Spanish quickly.
encourage give support and confidence to Teachers encourage students to travel.
fluent able to speak easily and well He is fluent in three languages.
make it affordable reduce the cost so people can pay Grants make it affordable.
confidence belief in your own ability Travel builds confidence.

2.1 Kids’ Smartphones

Do you think children under 12 should have their own smartphones? Explain your opinion.

Sample

In my opinion, children under twelve shouldn’t have their own smartphones. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, young children aren’t mature enough to handle the internet. They can easily see inappropriate content or talk to strangers online. For example, many parents report that their kids have been exposed to violent videos by accident.

Moreover, phones harm their development. Kids spend hours playing games instead of playing outside, which affects their health, sleep, and social skills. On the other hand, a phone allows parents to contact their child, but a simple basic phone can do that without the risks.

All in all, children should enjoy a childhood with real-life experiences. That’s why I firmly believe that smartphones should wait until the teenage years, and parents should always use parental controls.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence

social skills the ability to interact with others Play builds social skills.
real-life actual, not online Real-life friends matter.
parental controls settings that limit what children can see Turn on parental controls.

2.2 Social Media’s Effect

Does social media have a more positive or negative effect on society? Give your view.

Sample

In my opinion, social media has a more negative effect on society. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, it spreads misinformation very quickly. False news can reach millions of people before anyone checks it. For example, during the pandemic, many people believed dangerous rumours they found online.

Moreover, it harms mental health. Constantly comparing yourself to perfect photos causes anxiety and low self-esteem, especially among teenagers. On the other hand, social media connects families and gives a voice to ordinary people, but these benefits are outweighed by the damage of addiction and cyberbullying.

All in all, while it has some advantages, the negative effects are stronger. That’s why I firmly believe that society needs better rules and more digital literacy.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
misinformation false information Misinformation spreads fast.
mental health emotional and psychological wellbeing Protect your mental health.
self-esteem confidence in your own worth Low self-esteem hurts.
addiction inability to stop doing something Phone addiction is common.
digital literacy skill in using technology wisely Teach digital literacy.

2.3 Limiting Phone Time

Should people limit the time they spend on their phones? Why or why not?

Sample

In my opinion, people should definitely limit their phone time. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, too much screen time is bad for health. It causes eye strain, poor sleep, and back problems. For example, I used to scroll in bed for hours, and I woke up tired every morning.

Moreover, excessive phone use damages relationships. People sit together at a table and ignore each other, which is ironic, because phones are supposed to connect us. On the other hand, phones are useful for work and communication, but we can still set limits, such as no phones at meals or after ten at night.

All in all, using our phones mindfully gives us more time for hobbies, exercise, and real conversations. That’s why I firmly believe that everyone should practise a healthy digital balance.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
screen time time spent on devices Reduce your screen time.
ironic the opposite of what you’d expect It’s ironic.
mindfully with careful attention Eat mindfully.
digital balance a healthy use of technology Aim for digital balance.

2.4 AI at Work

Is artificial intelligence good for the workplace? Explain.

Sample

In my opinion, artificial intelligence is good for the workplace, if it’s used wisely. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, AI boosts productivity. It can handle repetitive tasks like data entry and scheduling, so employees can focus on creative work. For example, my company uses AI to summarize long reports, and it saves us hours every week.

Moreover, it helps reduce human error, especially in areas like medicine and finance. On the other hand, many people fear that AI will replace their jobs, but history shows that technology also creates new roles, and workers can upskill. Of course, companies must protect privacy and be transparent.

All in all, AI is a powerful tool that supports people rather than replaces them. That’s why I firmly believe that businesses should embrace it responsibly.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
boosts productivity increases how much work gets done Software boosts productivity.
upskill learn new skills Workers must upskill.
privacy freedom from being watched Protect customer privacy.

2.5 Sharing Online

Should people share their personal lives on social media? Give your opinion.

Sample

In my opinion, people should be careful about sharing their personal lives online. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, it threatens privacy and safety. Posting your address, your holiday plans, or photos of your children can attract thieves or scammers. For example, a friend’s home was robbed while she was posting photos from a beach vacation.

Moreover, anything you post can stay on the internet forever, and employers often check candidates’ profiles. On the other hand, sharing happy moments helps us stay in touch with loved ones, but we can do that in private groups instead of making everything public.

All in all, a bit of discretion protects our reputation and our families. That’s why I firmly believe that people should think twice before they post.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
privacy freedom from public attention I value my privacy.
scammers people who trick others to get money Scammers target seniors.
discretion careful judgment about what to say Use discretion online.

3.1 Working From Home

Is working from home better than working in an office? Explain your opinion.

Sample

In my opinion, working from home is better for most people. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, it saves time and money. There’s no commute, so you gain one or two hours a day, and you spend less on gas, lunch, and work clothes. For example, I now use that time for exercise, and I’m healthier.

Moreover, employees are often more productive at home, because there are fewer interruptions and they can organize their day. On the other hand, some people feel isolated at home, but they can still meet colleagues through video calls and occasional office days. A hybrid model can solve that.

All in all, remote work gives a better work-life balance and makes employees loyal. That’s why I firmly believe that companies should offer it whenever possible.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
commute the daily trip to work A long commute is tiring.

3.2 Four-Day Work Week

Should companies adopt a four-day work week? Why or why not?

Sample

In my opinion, companies should adopt a four-day work week. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, it improves wellbeing. With three days off, people have more time to rest, to be with family, and to take care of themselves, so they come back refreshed. For example, in several trials in Europe, employees reported less stress and fewer sick days.

Moreover, productivity often stays the same or even rises, because people concentrate better when time is limited. On the other hand, some businesses worry about customer service, but they can use rotating shifts to stay open every day.

All in all, a shorter week is a win for both employers and employees, because it attracts talent and reduces turnover. That’s why I firmly believe that it’s the future of work.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
wellbeing health and happiness Employee wellbeing matters.
rotating shifts work times that change in turn Nurses work rotating shifts.
turnover the rate at which workers leave Turnover is low.

3.3 Satisfaction vs Salary

Is job satisfaction more important than salary? Give your view.

Sample

In my opinion, job satisfaction is more important than salary. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, we spend a third of our lives at work, so if we hate our job, it affects our health and happiness. For example, my uncle earned a high salary in banking, but he was so unhappy and stressed that he finally quit.

Moreover, people who enjoy their work are more creative and more successful in the long run, so they often earn more later. On the other hand, money is necessary to pay bills and support a family, but once your basic needs are met, extra income brings little additional fulfillment.

All in all, a meaningful job with a fair salary is better than a high-paying job that makes you miserable. That’s why I firmly believe that satisfaction should come first.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
creative able to produce new ideas A creative team.
fulfillment satisfaction from achieving something Teaching gives me fulfillment.
meaningful important and worthwhile A meaningful career.

3.4 Retirement Age

Should the retirement age be raised? Explain.

Sample

In my opinion, the retirement age shouldn’t be raised. There are two main reasons why I think so.

First of all, many people have physically demanding jobs, such as construction or nursing, and working into their late sixties can damage their health. For example, my father is a mechanic, and his back already hurts at sixty.

Moreover, older workers who stay in their jobs block opportunities for young people, who struggle to find work. On the other hand, people live longer now, and pension systems face pressure, but the government can solve that by encouraging voluntary later retirement, with incentives, rather than forcing it.

All in all, retirement should be a reward for decades of work. That’s why I firmly believe that the retirement age should remain flexible, not higher.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
voluntary done by choice Voluntary retirement.
incentives rewards that encourage action Tax incentives.
reward something given for effort A well-earned reward.

3.5 Monitoring Employees

Should employers monitor employees’ internet use at work? Why or why not?

Sample

In my opinion, employers should monitor internet use only in limited ways. There are two main reasons why I think so.
