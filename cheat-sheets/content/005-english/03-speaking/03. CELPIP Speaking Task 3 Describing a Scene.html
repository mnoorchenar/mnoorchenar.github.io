CELPIP Speaking Task 3: Describing a Scene

1 Public Places and Outdoor Scenes

Topics: a park, a street, a beach, a playground, a market, a train or bus station.

General Template

In this picture, I can see [the place and the general mood]. In the foreground, [main people and what they are doing].

On the left / right, [other people or objects]. In the background, [the setting, buildings, weather].

It looks like [what the people are feeling or planning]. Overall, [the general atmosphere].

1.1 Park on a Sunny Day

A park on a sunny day with people walking dogs, children playing, a couple on a bench, and a vendor selling ice cream. Describe the scene in detail.

Sample

In this picture, I can see a lively park on a beautiful sunny day. In the foreground, a man and a woman are walking two playful dogs along the path, and the dogs seem very excited.

On the left, a group of children are running around and playing tag on the grass. Meanwhile, a young couple is sitting on a wooden bench, enjoying the weather and chatting. On the right, there’s an ice cream vendor with a colourful cart, and a small line of people is waiting.

In the background, there are tall green trees that provide shade.

It looks like everyone is relaxed and happy. Overall, it’s a cheerful weekend scene.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
playful full of fun and energy The playful puppy chased a ball.
vendor a person who sells things The vendor sold hot dogs.
shade a cool area out of the sun We sat in the shade.

1.2 Busy Beach

A busy beach with swimmers, people sunbathing, a volleyball game, and a lifeguard tower. Describe what you see.

Sample

In this picture, I can see a crowded beach on a hot summer day. In the foreground, several people are sunbathing on colourful towels, and one woman is putting on sunscreen.

On the left, a group of teenagers are playing volleyball, and one boy is jumping to hit the ball. Meanwhile, a few swimmers are splashing in the waves further out. On the right, there’s a tall lifeguard tower, and the lifeguard is watching the water carefully.

In the background, I can see a clear blue sky and a few sailboats on the horizon.

It looks like everyone is having a great time. Overall, the atmosphere is relaxed and energetic.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
sunbathing lying in the sun to get a tan She was sunbathing on the sand.
lifeguard tower a high seat for watching swimmers The lifeguard tower stood near the water.
energetic full of energy The kids were energetic.

1.3 Farmers Market

An outdoor farmers market with stalls, shoppers, fruits and vegetables, and musicians. Describe the scene.

Sample

In this picture, I can see a bustling farmers market on a bright morning. In the foreground, there’s a stall with piles of fresh tomatoes, apples, and carrots, and a smiling seller is handing a bag to a customer.

On the left, a few shoppers are carrying reusable bags and comparing the prices of vegetables. Meanwhile, on the right, two musicians are playing a guitar and a violin, and a small group has stopped to listen. In the background, I can see white tents lined up along the street.

It looks like the shoppers are enjoying the fresh produce and the music. Overall, the market has a warm, community feeling.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
bustling full of busy activity The street was bustling with shoppers.
reusable bags shopping bags that can be used many times I bring reusable bags to the store.
fresh produce newly picked fruits and vegetables The fresh produce looked delicious.

1.4 City Street at Rush Hour

A city street at rush hour with pedestrians, cars, a cyclist, and a coffee shop. Describe what is happening.

Sample

In this picture, I can see a busy city street during rush hour. In the foreground, many pedestrians are crossing the street, and most of them are wearing business clothes and carrying bags.

On the left, a cyclist is waiting at the red light, wearing a helmet. Meanwhile, the road is full of cars, and the traffic seems to be moving slowly. On the right, there’s a small coffee shop with a long queue of people waiting for their morning drinks.

In the background, tall office buildings line both sides of the street.

It looks like people are hurrying to work. Overall, the scene feels hectic, but it’s typical of a big city.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
crossing the street going from one side of the road to the other Children were crossing the street safely.
long queue a long line of people waiting There was a long queue at the bank.
hectic very busy and rushed My morning was hectic.
typical showing the usual features It’s a typical Monday morning.

1.5 Train Platform

A train platform with passengers waiting, a person checking a schedule, and a train arriving. Describe the scene.

Sample

In this picture, I can see a train platform in the early evening. In the foreground, a man in a grey coat is checking the timetable on the wall, and he looks a little worried.

On the left, a few passengers are standing behind the yellow line with suitcases and backpacks. Meanwhile, a woman is looking at her phone, and a young boy is holding his mother’s hand. On the right, a train is pulling into the station, and the doors are about to open.

In the background, there’s a large clock and a sign showing the platform number.

It looks like the passengers are getting ready to board. Overall, the scene is calm but a bit crowded, and everyone seems eager to get home.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
pulling into the station arriving at the station The train is pulling into the station now.
train platform the raised area where passengers wait for trains We waited on the train platform for ten minutes.
eager very keen and excited The kids were eager to leave.

On the left, two people are sitting at a small table with their laptops open, and they seem to be working. Meanwhile, behind the counter, another barista is pouring milk into a cup, making a latte. On the right, a couple is chatting over coffee and a slice of cake.

In the background, there’s a chalkboard menu on the wall and some green plants.

It looks like it’s a popular spot. Overall, the atmosphere is warm and inviting, and it smells of freshly brewed coffee.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
barista a person who makes coffee drinks The barista drew a heart in the foam.
inviting pleasant and attractive The café had an inviting look.
freshly brewed just made, for coffee or tea I love freshly brewed tea.

On the left, there’s a clothing store with a big red sign that says “50% Off,” and a small crowd is looking at the window. Meanwhile, on the right, a family of four is eating at the food court, and the kids are sharing a plate of fries. In the background, I can see an escalator and more stores on the upper floor.

It looks like it’s a weekend. Overall, the mall is lively and full of shoppers.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
bargain something bought for less than its usual price The jacket was a real bargain.
food court an area in a mall with many places to eat We had lunch at the food court.
lively full of energy The street was lively at night.

An airport terminal with travellers with luggage, an information desk, and a departures board. Describe what is going on.

Sample

In this picture, I can see a big airport terminal with many travellers. In the foreground, a man is pulling a suitcase and looking up at the departures board, probably checking his gate number.

On the left, there’s an information desk, and a woman in a uniform is explaining something to a family with two small kids. Meanwhile, on the right, a few passengers are sitting on benches near their luggage, and one of them is sleeping. In the background, I can see check-in counters and long lines of people.

It looks like the travellers are either waiting for flights or searching for directions. Overall, the scene is busy but orderly.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
departures board a screen showing flight times Check the departures board for your gate.
check-in counters desks where travellers register for flights and drop their bags The check-in counters open three hours before departure.
orderly neat and well organized The line was orderly.

Sample

In this picture, I can see a modern gym with a lot of equipment. In the foreground, a muscular man is lifting weights on a bench, and his face shows effort.

On the left, a personal trainer is helping a woman with her posture while she’s using a machine. Meanwhile, on the right, several people are running on treadmills in a row, and some are wearing headphones. In the background, I can see mirrors on the walls and a water fountain.

It looks like everyone is determined to stay fit. Overall, the gym feels energetic and motivating.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
lifting weights raising heavy objects for exercise He enjoys lifting weights every morning.
treadmills machines for running indoors The gym has ten treadmills.
determined firmly decided to achieve something She is determined to finish.
motivating giving you the desire to act The coach was motivating.

On the left, a large group of friends is sitting around a round table, and a waitress is carrying a birthday cake with candles toward them. Meanwhile, everyone seems ready to sing. On the right, a few diners are enjoying their meals, and the table is set with glasses and napkins.

In the background, there’s soft lighting and a bar with bottles on the shelves.

It looks like a celebration. Overall, the atmosphere is warm and festive.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence

festive joyful and suited to a party The festive room was full of balloons.
