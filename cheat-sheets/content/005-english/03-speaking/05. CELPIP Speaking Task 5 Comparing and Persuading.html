CELPIP Speaking Task 5: Comparing and Persuading

1 Housing and Places to Live

Topics: apartments, houses, locations, shared housing, renting versus buying.

General Template

I would strongly recommend [your choice]. First of all, [reason 1 with a benefit].

On top of that, [reason 2 or an example]. Admittedly, [the other option has one advantage], but [why yours is still better].

That’s why I really think [your choice] is [the better option for you].

1.1 Condo vs House

Your family is choosing between a condo downtown and a house in the suburbs. Choose one and persuade your family.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the condo downtown. First of all, it’s close to our jobs, schools, and shops, so we’d save hours every week on the commute. That means more family time.

On top of that, a condo needs very little maintenance. There’s no lawn to mow and no snow to shovel, because the building takes care of it. We’d also enjoy amenities like a gym and a pool.

Admittedly, the suburban house has a bigger yard, but we rarely use it, and we could visit a park nearby. Plus, downtown has restaurants, museums, and public transit right outside our door.

That’s why I really think the condo is the better option for our busy lifestyle.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
commute the daily trip to work His commute is short.
maintenance the work of keeping something in good condition The house needs regular maintenance.
amenities extra facilities in a building The amenities include a pool.
public transit buses and trains I take public transit.

1.2 Cheap vs Close Apartment

A friend is picking between two apartments, one cheaper and farther from work, one expensive and close. Persuade them to choose the one you prefer.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the cheaper apartment. First of all, the lower rent will save you a lot of money every month, and you can put that money toward savings or travel.

On top of that, being farther from work isn’t a big problem if there’s a good train or bus line. You could read, listen to podcasts, or relax during the ride. Furthermore, a cheaper place reduces your financial stress, which makes life much more enjoyable.

Admittedly, the expensive apartment is more convenient, but you’d be paying a premium for a few saved minutes, and an unexpected expense could put you under pressure.

That’s why I really think the cheaper apartment is the smarter investment for you.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
financial stress worry about money Rent can cause financial stress.
premium an extra amount paid for something You pay a premium for the view.
investment something that gives long-term value A good education is an investment.

1.3 Campus Home vs City Centre ⭐

You and a friend or your sister are looking for a place to live. One is near a university, cheaper, on the campus; the other is in the city centre, more expensive and close to many companies and workplaces. Choose one and persuade her.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 11 and Oct 10.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the place on the university campus. First of all, it’s much cheaper, so we could cut down on our monthly expenses and put aside some money for emergencies. The savings alone would be a big relief.

On top of that, we’d be a short walk from classes, the library and the gym, so we wouldn’t waste time in traffic. The campus is also quiet and safe, and it has a strong sense of community.

Admittedly, the city centre is close to many companies, but we can get around easily by bus, and we could look for internships online.

That’s why I really think the campus home is the better option for us. It’s affordable, convenient and perfect for our lifestyle.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
cut down on reduce We cut down on eating out.
put aside save for later She puts aside fifty dollars a week.
get around move from place to place It’s easy to get around by subway.
relief a feeling of comfort after worry It was a relief to find cheap rent.
strong sense of community a feeling of belonging to a group The neighbourhood has a strong sense of community.
lifestyle the way a person lives A student lifestyle is simple.

1.4 Living Alone vs Sharing

Your sister is choosing between living alone and sharing an apartment with friends. Persuade her.

Sample

I would strongly recommend sharing an apartment with friends. First of all, you’d split the rent and the bills, so you’d pay much less than you would on your own. That’s a huge help, especially at the start of your career.

On top of that, you’d have company, and you’d never feel lonely. Cooking dinners, watching movies, and chatting after work make life warmer. Also, friends can help each other in emergencies.

Admittedly, living alone gives you more privacy, but you can still enjoy personal space in your own bedroom, and you can agree on house rules to keep things peaceful.

That’s why I really think sharing is the better option. It’s affordable and fun.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
personal space privacy and room to be alone I need some personal space.
house rules agreed guidelines at home Our house rules are simple.
affordable not too expensive The apartment is affordable.

1.5 Renting vs Buying

Your parents are deciding between renting a large place and buying a smaller one. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend buying the smaller place. First of all, when you rent, you pay every month and own nothing. With a purchase, your payments build equity, so you’d own something valuable in the future.

On top of that, you’d have stability. A landlord can raise the rent or sell the building, but if you own your home, you decide how long you’ll stay. Also, property usually appreciates in value over time.

Admittedly, the large rental has more space, but at your age, you don’t need much room, and a smaller home is cheaper to heat and clean.

That’s why I really think buying is the wiser choice for your retirement.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
equity the value of the part of a property you own Equity grows as you pay your mortgage.
stability the state of being steady and secure A home gives stability.
appreciates increases in value The house appreciated by ten percent.
retirement the time of life after working They plan for retirement.

1.6 Town vs City

Your friend has to choose between a quiet town and a lively city. Persuade them to pick one.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the lively city. First of all, a city offers many more job opportunities, and you can find work in almost any field. You’d also meet people from different backgrounds.

On top of that, there’s always something to do, such as concerts, restaurants, festivals, and museums. Besides, public transportation makes it easy to get around without a car.

Admittedly, a quiet town is peaceful, but it can feel isolating, and you’d probably get bored after a few months. In a city, you can always find a vibrant community, and you can escape to nature on weekends.

That’s why I really think the city is the better option for you.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
opportunities chances for success The city has many opportunities.
public transportation buses, trains and subways Public transportation is efficient.
isolating making you feel alone A small village can be isolating.
vibrant full of life The area is vibrant at night.

1.7 Which City to Move To ⭐

You are moving to another city. Your choice is sunny and nice with a good work-life balance and cheaper rents; your family’s choice has very high rents, a more industrial area and very cold winters. Convince your family that yours is better.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 4 (several reports) and Oct 3.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the sunny city. First of all, the rent is much lower, so we could afford a bigger apartment and still save money every month. Housing is the biggest expense for any family, and this would take a load off our budget.

On top of that, the work-life balance there is excellent. People finish work early and spend time outdoors, which is great for our health. The mild winters would also spare us from months of snow and freezing temperatures.

Admittedly, the other city is bigger and offers more jobs, but it’s industrial, very expensive and quite cold. We would end up working harder just to pay the bills.

That’s why I really think the sunny city is the better option for all of us.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
take a load off reduce a burden Cheaper rent takes a load off my mind.
spare from save someone from an unpleasant experience The mild climate spares us from harsh winters.
end up finally be in a situation We ended up paying more.
afford have enough money to pay for We can’t afford a big house.
work-life balance a healthy balance between job and personal time Good work-life balance reduces stress.
spend time outdoors be outside in nature I like to spend time outdoors.

2 Shopping and Purchases

Topics: choosing a car, a phone, a laptop, furniture, appliances, gifts.

General Template

I would strongly recommend [your choice]. First of all, [reason 1 with a benefit].

On top of that, [reason 2 or an example]. Admittedly, [the other option has one advantage], but [why yours is still better].

That’s why I really think [your choice] is [the better option for you].

2.1 Small Car vs SUV

Your family is choosing between a small, fuel-efficient car and a large SUV. Choose one and persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the small, fuel-efficient car. First of all, gas prices are very high, and a small car uses much less fuel, so we’d save hundreds of dollars a year.

On top of that, it’s easier to drive and park in the city, especially on narrow streets. Also, it’s more environmentally friendly, because it produces fewer emissions, and the insurance is usually cheaper.

Admittedly, the SUV has more room, but we only travel with a few people most of the time, and for big trips, we can rent a larger vehicle. A small car is also less expensive to maintain.

That’s why I really think the small car is the more practical choice for our family.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
environmentally friendly not harmful to nature Bikes are environmentally friendly.
maintain keep in good condition It’s costly to maintain an SUV.
practical sensible and useful A practical car saves money.

2.2 Lightweight vs Powerful Laptop

Your friend is picking between two laptops, one lightweight and one with more power. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the lightweight laptop. First of all, it’s much easier to carry around, so you can take it to class, to a café, or on a trip without back pain.

On top of that, the battery life on lightweight models is usually longer, which means you won’t need to look for a power outlet all day. Also, you said you only use it for browsing, writing, and watching videos, so you don’t need extra processing power.

Admittedly, the powerful laptop is faster, but it’s heavy, noisy, and often more expensive, and you’d pay for performance you’ll never use.

That’s why I really think the lightweight laptop is the smarter choice for your lifestyle.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
battery life how long a device works without charging The battery life is twelve hours.
processing power the speed of a computer’s brain Games need more processing power.
smarter wiser A smarter choice saves time.

2.3 Stylish vs Comfortable Sofa

You are helping your parents choose a sofa, one stylish and one comfortable. Persuade them to pick one.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the comfortable sofa. First of all, you’ll sit on it every day, so comfort matters more than looks. A soft sofa helps you relax after a long day and is great for movie nights.

On top of that, it’s better for your health. A supportive sofa is good for your back, and you can even take a nap on it. Also, comfortable sofas are usually made with quality cushions that last for years.

Admittedly, the stylish sofa looks great, but the seat is hard, and guests won’t want to stay long. You can always add colourful pillows and a nice blanket to improve the look.

That’s why I really think the comfortable sofa is the better investment.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
supportive giving good physical support A supportive chair helps my back.
colourful bright and varied in colour A colourful rug brightens the room.
investment something that gives long-term value Good furniture is an investment.

2.4 New vs Second-Hand Furniture ⭐

You are furnishing a home and must choose between new furniture and cheaper antique or second-hand furniture. Persuade the other person to accept your choice.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported in the Oct 2 compiled list.

Sample

I would strongly recommend buying second-hand furniture. First of all, it costs far less, so we can furnish the whole apartment without going over our budget. We could pick up a solid wooden table for a fraction of the price of a new one.

On top of that, older pieces are usually better built, and many of them have a timeless style. Buying them is also kinder to the planet, because we keep good items out of the landfill.

Admittedly, new furniture comes with a warranty, but we can fix up any small scratches ourselves, and we’d enjoy the project.

That’s why I really think second-hand furniture is the smarter choice. It’s cheaper, sturdier and more sustainable, and we’ll end up with a home full of character.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
pick up buy cheaply or by chance I picked up a lamp at a garage sale.
fix up repair and improve He fixed up an old chair.
end up with finally have We ended up with two sofas.
furnish fill a home with furniture They furnished the flat with antiques.
timeless not going out of fashion A timeless design.
sustainable good for the environment in the long term Second-hand shopping is sustainable.

2.5 Jewellery vs Trip

Your brother needs a gift for his wife and is choosing between jewellery and a trip. Persuade him.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the trip. First of all, jewellery is nice, but a trip creates lasting memories that you’ll enjoy together for years. You’ll talk about it long after you come home.

On top of that, spending quality time together will strengthen your relationship. A weekend in a beautiful place, away from work, gives you a chance to relax, and she’ll feel truly valued.

Admittedly, jewellery is something she can wear every day, but she may not like the style you choose, and it can be costly. A trip can be planned according to your budget, and it’s more personal.

That’s why I really think the trip is the better gift. It’s an experience money can’t replace.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
lasting memories memories that stay for a long time The trip left lasting memories.
costly expensive A diamond ring is costly.
budget a plan for spending Stay within your budget.
experience something you live through Travel is a great experience.

2.6 Headphones vs Mug ⭐

A coworker got a new job. You and another coworker want to buy a gift, and there are two options: headphones and a mug. Choose one and persuade your coworker.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 8.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the headphones. First of all, they’re useful every day, whether our colleague is commuting, working or relaxing at home. A new job often involves long hours at a desk, so noise-cancelling headphones would help him tune out distractions.

On top of that, headphones feel like a premium gift, so we could chip in together and still stay within our budget. A mug is cute, but he probably already owns several.

Admittedly, a mug is cheaper and has a personal touch, but we could add on a small card with a warm message to make the headphones more personal.

That’s why I really think the headphones are the better option. They show we care about his success in the new job.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
tune out stop paying attention to I tune out the noise when I read.
chip in each give a share of money We chipped in for the gift.
add on include as an extra I’ll add on a card.
noise-cancelling blocking outside sound Noise-cancelling headphones are great for flights.
premium of high quality It’s a premium brand.
personal touch something that shows individual care A handwritten note adds a personal touch.

2.7 Two Smartphones

Your friend is choosing between two smartphones with different prices and features. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the cheaper smartphone. First of all, it still has all the main features you need, such as a good camera, a fast processor, and enough storage. There’s no reason to pay more.

On top of that, you’d save about three hundred dollars, which you could use for a protective case, headphones, or even a weekend trip. Also, new phones come out every year, so an expensive model loses its value quickly.

Admittedly, the pricier phone has a slightly better screen and some extra features, but you probably won’t notice the difference in daily life, and they’re not worth the extra cost.

That’s why I really think the cheaper phone offers better value for you.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
storage space for saving files I need more storage.
protective case a cover that prevents damage I bought a protective case.
extra cost additional money There’s no extra cost.

2.8 Wheelbarrows ⭐

You need a wheelbarrow. Option 1 is used, metal, with two wheels, free but delivery costs $40. Option 2 is lightweight plastic with one wheel and costs $65. Compare the two and persuade your sister or a friend to choose one.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 11 (several reports), Oct 10 and in undated reports.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the used metal wheelbarrow. First of all, even with the forty-dollar delivery fee, it costs less than the plastic one, and the metal frame is much more durable. It can carry heavy loads of soil or bricks without giving way.

On top of that, the two wheels make it more stable, so it won’t tip over on uneven ground. The plastic model might be lighter, but a single wheel is harder to keep balanced, especially when it’s full.

Admittedly, the metal one is heavier and used, but we can wipe down the rust and it will last for years.

That’s why I really think the metal wheelbarrow is the better option. It’s cheaper, sturdier and a better investment.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
give way break under pressure The old shelf gave way.
tip over fall on its side The cart tipped over.
wipe down clean by wiping Wipe down the tools after use.
more durable able to last a long time Steel is more durable than plastic.
keep balanced stay steady It’s hard to keep balanced on one wheel.
investment something bought for long-term value Good tools are an investment.

2.9 Snow Removal Equipment ⭐

Your family wants to buy equipment to clear snow from the driveway. Compare the options (for example a plastic or wooden shovel, a walk-behind snow blower, or a small snowplow) and persuade a family member that your choice is better.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 9, Oct 8 (several reports) and Oct 7.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the walk-behind snow blower. First of all, it clears a driveway in minutes, while a shovel takes an hour of exhausting work. After a heavy snowfall, that saves a lot of energy, and we won’t have to put up with sore backs.

On top of that, it costs four hundred dollars, which is far cheaper than the enclosed-cab snowplow at seven hundred. It’s also easy to store in the garage, and it will pay for itself over the winters.

Admittedly, the plastic shovel is the cheapest, but it’s hard to keep up with a big storm, and shovelling can lead to injuries. The plow is comfortable, but it’s excessive for an ordinary driveway.

That’s why I really think the snow blower is the better option. It’s efficient, affordable and perfect for our needs.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
pay for itself earn back its cost A good coat pays for itself.
keep up with stay at the same level as I can’t keep up with the housework.
heavy snowfall a lot of snow Heavy snowfall closed the roads.
store keep for later use We store tools in the shed.
excessive more than necessary The price is excessive.
efficient working well without wasting time or effort A very efficient machine.
put up with tolerate I can’t put up with the noise.

Admittedly, the planner costs a few dollars more, but it is a practical gift that she’ll use every day.

That’s why I really think the planner is the better option. It shows that we want her to get off to a good start at university.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
keep track of follow and remember I keep track of my expenses.

practical useful in real life A practical present.
get off to a good start begin well He got off to a good start at work.

2.11 Washing Machines ⭐

You are choosing between two washing machines. Compare them and persuade a family member to buy the one you prefer.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported in the Oct 2 compiled list.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the front-loading washing machine. First of all, it is energy-efficient, so it will bring down our electricity and water bills every month. Over ten years, the savings will be significant.

On top of that, it cleans clothes more gently, so our sweaters and jeans will last longer. It’s also quieter and has a large capacity, which is perfect for a family that does laundry several times a week.

Admittedly, the top-loading model is cheaper at first, but it uses more water, and we would end up paying more in the long run.

That’s why I really think the front-loader is the better option. It’s a smart purchase that will pay off and hold up for years.

Vocabulary

2.12 Choosing a Camera ⭐

You need a camera. Compare an inexpensive easy-to-use video camera, a professional camera and an older camera, and persuade a friend to choose one.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported in the Oct 2 compiled list.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the easy-to-use video camera. First of all, it’s affordable and simple, so you can get the hang of it in a day. You don’t need to read a thick manual or fiddle with complicated settings.

On top of that, it’s light and compact, so you can carry it on trips and film on the go. The picture quality is good enough for family videos and social media.

Admittedly, a professional camera takes sharper images, but it’s expensive, heavy and takes months to master. An older camera may look charming, but it could break down at any moment.

That’s why I really think the easy-to-use camera is the better option. It’s good value for money, and you’ll actually enjoy using it.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence

value for money good quality for the price This is great value for money.

2.13 Summer Running Shoes ⭐

You are choosing shoes for summer running. Compare two pairs and persuade a friend which pair to buy.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported in the Oct 2 compiled list.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the lightweight mesh running shoes. First of all, they’re breathable, so your feet stay cool even on a hot day. Heavy, thick shoes can make your feet sweat and wear you out quickly.

On top of that, the soles have excellent cushioning, which protects your knees on hard pavement. They also dry fast if you get caught in a summer rainstorm.

Admittedly, the other pair looks stylish and lasts longer, but it’s much warmer and heavier, and it would weigh you down on long runs.

That’s why I really think the mesh shoes are the better option. They’re comfortable, practical and ideal if you want to work up to a ten-kilometre race this summer.

Vocabulary

work up to gradually reach a higher level She worked up to a marathon.
breathable allowing air to pass through A breathable fabric.
