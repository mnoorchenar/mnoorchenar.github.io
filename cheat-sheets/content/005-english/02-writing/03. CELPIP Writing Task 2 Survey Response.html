1.1 Garden vs Playground

Shared Outdoor Space Survey: Should an empty area become a community garden or a playground for children? Explain.

Sample

In my opinion, the empty area should become a community garden. The main reason is that a garden brings together people of all ages, while a playground mostly serves families with young children. For example, in my old neighbourhood, a small garden gave seniors, teenagers, and parents a reason to chat every weekend.

In addition, a garden provides fresh produce at a low cost. Many residents live in apartments without a yard, so they could grow vegetables and herbs and share the harvest. As a result, families would cut down on grocery costs and eat more healthily. A garden would also make the area greener and more sustainable.

Some people may argue that children need a safe place to play. However, the main park already has a playground, and a garden can include a small corner where kids can play. Volunteers could even set up weekend workshops that teach children where food comes from.

For these reasons, I strongly believe that a community garden is the better choice for our neighbourhood.

1.2 Soft vs Adventure Playground ⭐

The city is building a new playground. Option A: A soft playground for young children. Option B: An adventure playground for older children. Which do you prefer? Explain.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported in the Oct 2 compiled list.

Sample

In my opinion, the city should build a soft playground for young children. The main reason is that young children are the group most at risk of injury. For example, my niece once fell on a hard surface and ended up in the emergency room. The injury kept her away from school for two weeks, and her parents were very worried.

In addition, soft surfaces and low equipment give parents peace of mind, because they can keep an eye on their children easily. Older siblings who look after younger ones will feel less worried too. As a result, more families would visit. The equipment also helps toddlers develop motor skills and is age-appropriate.

Some people may argue that older children need more challenge. However, they can already use sports courts, and the city can set up a climbing wall in another park later. Both age groups deserve good facilities, but the youngest need protection first.

For these reasons, I support the soft playground.
