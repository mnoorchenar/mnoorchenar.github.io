CELPIP Writing Task 1: Email

1 Complaints and Problems

Topics: faulty or wrong product, poor service, delayed or lost delivery, billing errors, cancelled flights or bookings, misleading advertising, unfair fees.

General Template

Subject: [Problem] and [order or account number]

Dear [Name or Team],

I am writing to complain about [the problem and when it happened]. [Details: what went wrong, how it affected you].

Furthermore, [one more detail or what you already did]. As a result, [the effect on you].

I would like you to [your request: refund, replacement, correction, compensation]. I would appreciate it if you could reply within [number] days.

I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely, [Your name]

1.1 Damaged Laptop

You bought a laptop online and it arrived damaged. Write an email to the company explaining the problem and asking for a replacement or refund.

Sample

Subject: Damaged Laptop Received – Request for Replacement

Dear Customer Service Team,

I am writing to complain about the laptop I ordered from your website on March 12 (order number 48213). Unfortunately, the package arrived with a cracked screen and a dented corner, so the device cannot be used at all.

Upon receiving the package, I took photographs of the damage and kept the original box. Furthermore, I tried to turn on the laptop, but it failed to start. I believe it was mishandled during delivery, since the packaging was also crushed.

As a result, I would like you to send a replacement as soon as possible. However, if the same model is no longer in stock, I would opt for a full refund to my original payment method. Please also let me know how to send back the damaged laptop and whether you will cover the return shipping costs.

I would appreciate it if you could reply within three business days, as I rely on it for work. I have attached the photographs and my proof of purchase.

I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely,

Sara Ahmadi

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
replacement a new item given in place of a faulty one The store promised a replacement within a week.
cracked screen a display with visible breaks in the glass The phone fell, and now it has a cracked screen.
dented pushed inward by an impact The delivery driver dropped the box, and the corner was dented.
mishandled treated carelessly The fragile package was clearly mishandled in transport.
refund money returned after a purchase is cancelled I asked for a full refund because the product was defective.
return shipping the cost of sending an item back The company agreed to pay the return shipping.
proof of purchase a receipt showing you bought something You must show proof of purchase to exchange an item.
no longer in stock not available to buy any more Sorry, the red model is no longer in stock.
crushed badly squashed by pressure The box arrived crushed on one side.
original payment method the card or account used to pay The refund will go to your original payment method.
turn on to start a device I could not turn on the laptop.
send back to return something to the sender Please send back the damaged item within ten days.
rely on to depend on I rely on my laptop for work every day.
opt for to choose Most customers opt for a refund.

1.2 Faulty TV from a Department Store ⭐

You recently bought a TV from a department store and have many problems using it. Write an email to customer service explaining how you paid, describing the problems, and requesting what they should do to make up for it.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 11 and 10.

Sample

Subject: Faulty TV Purchased on October 3 – Request for Compensation

Dear Customer Service Team,

I am writing to complain about the 55-inch television I bought at your downtown store on October 3. I paid by credit card, and my receipt number is 20418. Unfortunately, the TV has been malfunctioning since the first day.

The screen flickers every few minutes, the sound often cuts out, and the remote control stops working after a short time. Furthermore, I went through the troubleshooting steps in the manual twice, but nothing helped. As a result, I cannot enjoy the TV that I paid a lot for.

I would like you to replace the television with a working one. However, if that is not possible, I would settle for a full refund. A gift voucher would also be a fair way to make up for the inconvenience. Please let me know whether a technician could look into the problem at my home.

I would appreciate it if you could reply within three business days. I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely,

Daniel Moreau

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
malfunctioning not working properly The malfunctioning oven stopped heating.
flickers shines or moves with an unsteady light The screen flickers whenever I change the channel.
refund money returned after a purchase is cancelled I asked for a full refund because the product was defective.
gift voucher a card that can be exchanged for goods in a store They offered me a gift voucher as an apology.
go through to check or follow step by step I went through the instructions twice.
settle for to accept something less than ideal I would settle for a partial refund.
make up for to compensate for The discount makes up for the delay.
look into to investigate The manager promised to look into the issue.

1.3 Laptop Refund Not Received ⭐

A refund promised within two weeks for a returned laptop has not arrived. Write an email to customer service explaining the situation and asking them to solve it.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported in the Oct 2 compiled list.

Sample

Subject: Missing Refund for Returned Laptop – Order 77310

Dear Customer Service Team,

I am writing to follow up on the refund for a laptop I returned on May 2. Your representative promised that the money would reach my account within two weeks. It is now almost five weeks, and I still have not received anything.

I sent back the laptop by courier, and the tracking page confirms that it arrived at your warehouse on May 6. Furthermore, I have called twice, but each time I was told that the refund was “being processed”. I have already had to chase up this matter twice. As a result, the outstanding amount of $950 is causing me real financial stress.

I would like you to process the refund immediately and send me a written confirmation once it is done. Otherwise, I will have to dispute the charge with my credit card company. I trust that your company will follow through on its promise.

I would appreciate it if you could reply within two business days. I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely,

Elena Petrova

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
outstanding still unpaid or unresolved There is an outstanding balance on my account.
process to handle an official request or payment It takes five days to process a refund.
confirmation official proof that something has been done I received a confirmation email.
send back to return something to the sender Please send back the damaged item within ten days.
chase up to follow up on something that is late I had to chase up the payment.
follow through on to do what you promised The company followed through on its offer.

1.4 Poor Restaurant Service

A restaurant gave you poor service at a family dinner. Write an email to the manager describing what happened and what you expect.

Sample

Subject: Poor Service During Our Family Dinner on Saturday

Dear Mr. Thompson,

I am writing to express my disappointment with the service my family received at your restaurant last Saturday evening. We had booked a table for six to celebrate my parents’ anniversary, so we were hoping for a memorable night.

Sadly, the evening did not go as planned. We waited for our appetizers for forty minutes, and two main dishes arrived lukewarm. Moreover, our server seemed inattentive and forgot our drink order twice. When I politely brought up the issue, a staff member simply shrugged.

As a result, the celebration was ruined. I would like you to look into how your team handled our table. I also expect a partial refund for the meal and a sincere apology from the staff involved. In addition, a complimentary dessert on our next visit would show that you value your customers.

I would be grateful if you could reply by the end of the week. We have been loyal guests for years, and I hope to keep dining with you.

Sincerely,

Maria Lopez

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
appetizers small dishes served before the main course We ordered two appetizers to share.
lukewarm only slightly warm, not hot The soup was lukewarm when it arrived.
inattentive not paying enough attention The inattentive waiter ignored our requests.
refund money given back I asked for a refund for the cold meal.
complimentary given free of charge The hotel offers a complimentary breakfast.
memorable worth remembering It was a memorable evening for everyone.
sincere apology a genuine statement of regret The manager offered a sincere apology.
loyal consistently supporting a business We are loyal customers of this bakery.
wait for to stay until something arrives or happens We waited for our food for an hour.
hope for to want and expect something good We were hoping for a quiet evening.
bring up to mention a subject I brought up the problem with the manager.
look into to investigate Please look into this complaint.

1.5 Loud Music at a Restaurant ⭐

A restaurant you often visit replaced its usual music with loud heavy rock. Write an email to the manager explaining the problem and suggesting a solution.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported in the Oct 2 compiled list.

Sample

Subject: Loud Music During My Visit on Friday

Dear Manager,

I am writing to share my concern about the music at your restaurant. I am a regular customer, and I always look forward to the relaxed ambiance and soft jazz. On Friday evening, however, loud heavy rock replaced it.

The music was so loud that my friend and I had to shout to hear each other. Furthermore, an older couple at the next table asked a waiter to turn down the volume, but the request was ignored. As a result, we cut our dinner short and ended up leaving before dessert.

I would like you to bring back the previous style of music, at least during dinner hours. Alternatively, you could set aside one night a week for livelier music. A moderate volume would let guests enjoy their meals and conversation.

I would appreciate it if you could let me know your decision. I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely,

Julien Martin

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
regular customer a person who often buys from or visits the same business She is a regular customer at the café.
ambiance the atmosphere of a place The restaurant has a warm ambiance.
volume how loud a sound is Please lower the volume.
look forward to to wait for something with pleasure I look forward to our weekly lunch.
turn down to reduce the volume or level Could you turn down the music?
end up to finally become or do something The project ended up costing more than expected.
set aside to reserve for a purpose The school set aside funds for new equipment.

1.6 Unreliable Internet

Your internet service has been unreliable for weeks. Write an email to the provider explaining the issue and requesting compensation.

Sample

Subject: Unreliable Internet Service – Request for Compensation

Dear Customer Support Team,

I am writing to bring to your attention the ongoing problems with my home internet connection (account number 70915). For the past three weeks, the service has been unreliable, and I experience frequent outages every day.

I work remotely and depend on a stable connection, so this has seriously affected my job. I missed an important video meeting last Tuesday because the connection dropped repeatedly. Furthermore, I have called your helpline three times, but the problem has not been resolved. Each technician promised a follow-up visit, yet nobody has turned up.

Consequently, I would like a technician to visit this week to sort out the problem. In addition, I am requesting compensation in the form of a credit on my next bill, since I have paid the full price for a service I have not received.

I would appreciate your prompt attention to this matter. If the issue continues, I will have to consider switching to another provider.

Thank you for your time and assistance.

Yours sincerely,

David Chen

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
unreliable not working consistently My unreliable connection drops every hour.
compensation payment for a loss or inconvenience She received compensation for the delay.
outages periods when a service stops working The storm caused several power outages.
resolved solved or settled The issue was resolved after two days.
follow-up a later action to check or finish something The company never made a follow-up call.
provider a company that supplies a service I am changing my internet provider.
stable connection a reliable link that does not drop I need a stable connection for video calls.
helpline a phone service for customer support I called the helpline twice this week.
credit on my next bill an amount taken off a future bill The company gave me a credit on my next bill.
turn up to arrive or appear The technician never turned up.
switch to to change to something else I am thinking of switching to another company.
sort out to solve a problem Can you sort out this issue today?
depend on to need and rely on I depend on the internet for my job.

1.7 Double Gym Charge

A gym membership was charged twice. Write an email to customer service asking for a correction.

Sample

Subject: Duplicate Charge on My Membership Account

Dear Customer Service Team,

I am writing to inform you of an error on my gym membership account (member number 55102). When I went through my bank statement this morning, I noticed that I had been charged twice for my monthly fee of $45 on June 1. I have attached a copy of my statement showing both transactions.

I have been a member for two years, and this has never happened before. The second payment left my account almost empty, which caused me some inconvenience.

As this is clearly a billing error, I would like the extra $45 to be reimbursed to my credit card as soon as possible. Additionally, please confirm in writing that my account has been corrected, to make sure the mistake does not happen again next month.

However, I do not wish to cancel my membership, because I enjoy your facilities and classes. Therefore, I hope we can sort out this matter quickly.

Thank you in advance for your help. Please get in touch with me by email or at 555-0147 if you need more information.

Sincerely,

Julia Martin

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
membership the right to use a club or service My gym membership renews every month.

charged twice billed two times for the same thing I was charged twice for one coffee.

reimbursed paid back The company reimbursed me for the extra fee.
monthly fee a fixed amount paid every month The monthly fee includes all classes.
inconvenience trouble or difficulty caused to someone We apologize for the inconvenience.

I trust that you will resolve this matter promptly. I have shopped with you for years, and I hope to stick with you.

Sincerely,

Karen Walsh

1.9 Order Five Days Late ⭐

You ordered something from a store, and the manager said it would arrive within two days. It has been five days and you have not received it. Write an email to the manager explaining the situation, describing how the delay has affected you, and asking the manager to resolve the problem.

Recent exam, October 2026: reported on Oct 4.

Sample

Subject: Order 55302 Still Not Delivered

Dear Mr. Patel,

I am writing to express my concern about my order (number 55302), which I placed on September 28. When I called the store, you personally told me that it would arrive within two days. Five days have passed, and I have not received anything.

This delay has caused real inconvenience. The order contains a printer that I need to print out documents for a job application due this Friday. I also tried twice to get hold of your delivery team without success. As a result, I have had to put off the application and may miss the deadline.

I would like you to look into the matter and tell me where my package is. However, if it cannot arrive by Thursday, I would prefer to cancel the order and receive a full refund. In addition, free expedited shipping on my next order would be appreciated.

I would appreciate it if you could reply by tomorrow. I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely,

Karim Haddad

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
inconvenience trouble or difficulty caused to someone We apologize for the inconvenience.
deadline the latest time by which something must be done The deadline for applications is Friday.
