1 Community Spaces and Local Decisions

Topics: parks, libraries, community centres, programs, parking, traffic, public transit, local events, volunteering, public money.

General Template

In my opinion, [your chosen option] would be the better choice for [the community]. The main reason is that [reason 1]. For example, [a specific example or short story].

In addition, [reason 2 with detail]. As a result, [the benefit for residents].

Some people may argue that [the other option has value]. However, [why your choice is still stronger].

For these reasons, I strongly believe that [your chosen option] is the best choice.

1.1 Garden vs Playground

Shared Outdoor Space Survey: Should an empty area become a community garden or a playground for children? Explain.

Sample

In my opinion, the empty area should become a community garden. The main reason is that a garden brings people of all ages together, while a playground mostly serves families with young children. For example, in my old neighbourhood, a small garden gave seniors, teenagers, and parents a reason to chat every weekend.

In addition, a garden provides fresh produce at a low cost. Many residents live in apartments without a yard, so they could grow vegetables and herbs and share the harvest. As a result, families would save money on groceries and eat more healthily. A garden would also make the area greener and more sustainable.

Some people may argue that children need a safe place to play. However, the main park already has a playground, and a garden can include a small corner where kids can play. Volunteers could even run weekend workshops that teach children where food comes from.

For these reasons, I strongly believe that a community garden is the better choice for our neighbourhood.

Word Meaning Example sentence
community garden a shared plot where residents grow plants together Our community garden has a plot for every apartment floor.
fresh produce newly picked fruit and vegetables The market sells fresh produce every Saturday.
harvest the crop gathered when it is ready We shared the harvest of tomatoes with our neighbours.
sustainable able to continue without harming the environment Growing food locally is a sustainable habit.
playground an outdoor area with equipment for children The new playground has swings and a slide.

1.2 Digital Resources vs Event Space

Library Renovation Survey: Should the library expand digital and technology resources or create a community event space? Give reasons.

Sample

I believe that the library should expand its digital resources and technology. The main reason is that most people now look for information online, and many residents cannot afford their own devices. For example, a student without a laptop at home could use the library’s computers to finish assignments and apply for jobs.

Furthermore, e-books and online databases are available around the clock. A busy worker can borrow a novel at midnight without visiting the building. As a result, the library would reach more people, including those with limited mobility who find it hard to travel. New tools such as 3D printers could also attract young people and spark their creativity.

Admittedly, an event space would be nice for meetings and workshops. However, those activities can take place in schools or the community centre, which already have large rooms. Technology, on the other hand, is expensive for individuals but affordable when it is shared by everyone.

For these reasons, I would choose to invest in digital and technology resources.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
digital resources materials such as e-books and databases used on a device The library offers many digital resources for students.
e-books books read on a screen I borrow e-books on my tablet during my commute.
limited mobility difficulty moving around easily Home delivery helps people with limited mobility.
affordable not too expensive The library gives affordable access to computers.

1.3 Youth Sports vs Adult Classes

Community Centre Programming Survey: Should it offer youth sports programs for teenagers or adult education classes? Support your choice.

Sample

From my point of view, the community centre should offer youth sports programs for teenagers. The main reason is that teenagers need a healthy way to spend their free time. Without organized activities, many of them stay indoors on their phones or hang out on the streets. For example, a basketball league in my town kept dozens of teens busy after school and gave them a sense of belonging.

Moreover, sports teach valuable life skills, such as teamwork, discipline, and leadership. Teens who play on a team learn to handle both winning and losing. As a result, they often perform better at school and develop more self-confidence. Regular exercise also reduces stress and improves physical fitness.

Some people may argue that adult education classes help residents find better jobs. However, adults can already take courses at colleges and online, whereas teenagers have far fewer affordable options. Investing in young people also benefits the whole community in the long run.

For these reasons, I strongly support youth sports programs.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
hang out to spend time somewhere without a plan Teenagers like to hang out at the mall.
leadership the ability to guide a group Captains learn leadership by organizing practices.
self-confidence belief in your own abilities Winning the match boosted her self-confidence.
physical fitness good health from regular exercise Swimming improves physical fitness.

1.4 Food Trucks in the Park

Park Food Vendor Survey: Should the city allow food trucks in the park on weekends or keep the park free of vendors? Explain.

Sample

In my opinion, the city should allow food trucks in the park on weekends. The main reason is that the park is busiest on weekends, and many families stay for hours. Having food nearby means that people do not have to leave and lose their picnic spot. For example, at a park near my home, a few trucks selling tacos and ice cream made weekends feel like a small festival.

In addition, food trucks support local small businesses. Many entrepreneurs cannot afford a restaurant, so a truck gives them a chance to start. As a result, the city would see more jobs and more local spending. The trucks could also pay a small permit fee that helps maintain the park.

Some people may argue that vendors create noise and litter. However, the city can solve this problem with simple rules, such as limiting the number of trucks, setting clear hours, and placing extra recycling bins nearby.

For these reasons, I believe that weekend food trucks would improve the park for everyone.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
entrepreneurs people who start their own businesses Young entrepreneurs often begin with a small food truck.
local spending money spent within the community Tourism increases local spending.
permit fee a payment for official permission Each truck pays a permit fee to the city.

1.5 Visitor Parking

Visitor Parking Survey: Should an apartment building assign one visitor space per apartment or keep first-come, first-served parking? Give reasons.

Sample

I would say that the building should keep first-come, first-served parking. The main reason is that it is more flexible and makes better use of limited space. Most days, many apartments have no visitors at all, so assigned spaces would sit empty while other guests circle the lot looking for a spot. For example, my cousin lives in a building with assigned spaces, and I often see five or six empty spots that nobody is allowed to use.

In addition, an assignment system would be hard to manage. Someone would have to keep a list, issue passes, and handle complaints. As a result, the building would spend more money and staff time on administration instead of repairs.

Some people may argue that assigned spaces are fairer for every resident. However, a simple time limit, such as four hours per visit, can stop people from abusing the system. The building could also post clear signs and tow vehicles that stay too long.

For these reasons, I strongly support keeping the current system.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
staff time the hours employees spend on a task Checking passes would take a lot of staff time.
administration the work of managing and organizing something The administration of the lot is costly.
abusing using something unfairly or wrongly Some drivers are abusing the free parking.
tow to pull a vehicle away with a truck The building will tow any car parked illegally.

1.6 Traffic Lights vs Pedestrian Zone

Downtown Intersection Survey: Should the city install new traffic lights or create a pedestrian-only zone? Explain.

Sample

From my perspective, the city should create a pedestrian-only zone downtown. The main reason is that it would make the area much safer. Busy intersections are dangerous because cars, bikes, and walkers all compete for the same space. For example, when a city centre in Europe that I visited closed its main street to cars, accidents dropped and people felt more relaxed while walking.

Moreover, a pedestrian zone would be good for local business. People who walk slowly are more likely to stop at cafés, browse shops, and enjoy street performers. As a result, downtown would become a lively place that attracts both residents and tourists. It would also reduce traffic noise and air pollution.

Some people may argue that traffic lights are cheaper and keep cars moving. However, lights only manage the problem instead of solving it, and long lines of idling cars still produce exhaust. Drivers can use nearby streets or parking garages on the edge of the zone.

For these reasons, I firmly believe that a pedestrian-only zone is the better investment.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
pedestrian-only zone an area closed to vehicles so people can walk freely The pedestrian-only zone is full of shoppers on Sundays.
street performers artists who entertain people in public places Street performers play music near the fountain.
pollution harmful substances in the air, water, or soil Fewer cars means less pollution.
exhaust gas released by a vehicle’s engine The exhaust from the buses made the air unpleasant.
parking garages buildings where cars are parked I left my car in one of the parking garages near the station.

1.7 Public Transit vs Roads

Should your city spend more money on public transit or on roads? Give reasons.

Sample

In my opinion, my city should spend more money on public transit. The main reason is that good transit helps far more people than wider roads do. A single bus can carry fifty passengers, while the same space on the road holds only a few cars. For example, when my city added a new train line, thousands of commuters switched from driving, and rush hour became much calmer.

In addition, public transit is affordable and fair. Not everyone owns a car, especially students, seniors, and people on a tight budget. As a result, reliable buses and trains give them access to jobs, schools, and hospitals. Transit also reduces greenhouse gas emissions, which helps protect the environment.

Some people may argue that roads are necessary for delivery trucks and emergency vehicles. However, fewer private cars on the road would actually make those trips faster. Building more lanes often brings more congestion because new drivers fill them quickly.

For these reasons, I strongly believe that public transit deserves more funding.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
affordable not too expensive A monthly pass makes the bus affordable.
tight budget very little money to spend Students on a tight budget rely on transit.
greenhouse gas emissions gases released into the air that warm the planet Electric buses reduce greenhouse gas emissions.

funding money provided for a specific purpose The city increased funding for bus routes.

1.9 Bike Lanes vs Road Repairs

Your city has extra funds. Should it build more bike lanes or repair old roads? Explain.

Sample

In my opinion, the city should use the extra funds to repair old roads. The main reason is that damaged roads affect everyone, whether they drive, ride a bus, or cycle. Potholes can damage tires, cause accidents, and make travel slower. For example, last winter, a large pothole on my street cost me two hundred dollars in car repairs, and several of my neighbours had the same problem.

In addition, repairing roads is a smart long-term investment. Small repairs now are far cheaper than rebuilding a road after years of neglect. As a result, the city would save money in the future and avoid costly emergency work. Good roads also help ambulances, fire trucks, and delivery vehicles reach their destinations quickly.

Some people may argue that bike lanes encourage healthy and eco-friendly commuting. However, cyclists also need smooth surfaces, so a lane on a broken road is not safe. Fixing the roads first would benefit cyclists and drivers alike, and new bike lanes could be added later.

For these reasons, I firmly support repairing the old roads.

costly expensive Delaying repairs can be costly.

As a result, libraries strengthen the whole community and help people improve their lives. They are also among the few places where people can spend time without having to buy anything.

Some people may argue that sports facilities keep people active and healthy. However, many private gyms and clubs already exist, and people can exercise in parks for free. Libraries, in contrast, have no real substitute, and their public funding guarantees access for all.

For these reasons, I believe that libraries should be the priority.

1.12 Museum Fees

Should museums and public attractions be free or charge a small fee? Support your choice.

Sample

In my opinion, museums and public attractions should charge a small fee. The main reason is that these places are expensive to run. As a result, a modest price encourages people to plan their visit and enjoy it properly. The fee can also be low enough that families remain able to attend, especially if the museum offers discounts for students and seniors.

Some people may argue that culture should be free for everyone. However, the museum could still hold one free day each month to ensure accessibility. That balance would keep the attraction financially stable while protecting the public’s right to enjoy heritage.

For these reasons, I support a small, fair entry fee.

1.15 Security Cameras

Should the city install security cameras in public spaces or protect privacy by not using them? Give reasons.

Sample

In my opinion, the city should install security cameras in public spaces. The main reason is that cameras help prevent crime and protect residents. When criminals know they are being recorded, they are less likely to commit vandalism or theft. For example, in a train station near my home, the number of thefts dropped sharply after cameras were installed.

In addition, camera footage helps police solve crimes faster. Officers can identify suspects, confirm what happened, and find missing people more quickly. Some people may argue that cameras threaten privacy. However, public spaces are, by definition, places where people can already be seen by anyone. The city can also set strict rules, such as limiting who can view the footage and deleting it after thirty days. These safeguards would keep personal information confidential while still providing surveillance where it matters.

For these reasons, I support using security cameras in public spaces.

safeguards rules or measures that protect against harm Strong safeguards protect people’s privacy.

2.1 Flexible Hours vs 9-to-5

Workplace Schedule Survey: Should a company keep fixed 9-to-5 hours or allow flexible start and finish times? Explain.

Sample

In my opinion, the company should allow flexible hours. The main reason is that people have different energy levels and responsibilities. Some employees do their best work early in the morning, while others are sharper in the afternoon.
