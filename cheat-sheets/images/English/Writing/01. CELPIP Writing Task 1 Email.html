CELPIP Writing Task 1: Email

1 Complaints and Problems

Topics: faulty or wrong product, poor service, delayed or lost delivery, billing errors, cancelled flights or bookings, misleading advertising, unfair fees.

General Template

Subject: [Problem] and [order or account number]

Dear [Name or Team],

I am writing to complain about [the problem and when it happened]. [Details: what went wrong, how it affected you].

Furthermore, [one more detail or what you already did]. As a result, [the effect on you].

I would like you to [your request: refund, replacement, correction, compensation]. I would appreciate it if you could reply within [number] days.

I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely, [Your name]

1.1 Damaged Laptop

You bought a laptop online and it arrived damaged. Write an email to the company explaining the problem and asking for a replacement or refund.

Sample

Subject: Damaged Laptop Received – Request for Replacement

Dear Customer Service Team,

I am writing to complain about the laptop I ordered from your website on March 12 (order number 48213). Unfortunately, the package arrived with a cracked screen and a dented corner, so the device cannot be used at all.

Upon receiving the package, I took photographs of the damage and kept the original box. Furthermore, I tried to turn the laptop on, but it failed to start. I believe it was mishandled during delivery, since the packaging was also crushed.

As a result, I would like you to send a replacement as soon as possible. However, if the same model is no longer in stock, I would prefer a full refund to my original payment method. Please also let me know how to send back the damaged laptop and whether you will cover the return shipping costs.

I would appreciate it if you could reply within three business days, as I need the laptop for work. I have attached the photographs and my proof of purchase.

I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely,

Sara Ahmadi

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
replacement a new item given in place of a faulty one The store promised a replacement within a week.
cracked screen a display with visible breaks in the glass The phone fell, and now it has a cracked screen.
dented pushed inward by an impact The delivery driver dropped the box, and the corner was dented.
mishandled treated carelessly The fragile package was clearly mishandled in transport.
refund money returned after a purchase is cancelled I asked for a full refund because the product was defective.
return shipping the cost of sending an item back The company agreed to pay the return shipping.
proof of purchase a receipt showing you bought something You must show proof of purchase to exchange an item.

1.2 Poor Restaurant Service

A restaurant gave you poor service at a family dinner. Write an email to the manager describing what happened and what you expect.

Sample

Subject: Poor Service During Our Family Dinner on Saturday

Dear Mr. Thompson,

I am writing to express my disappointment with the service my family received at your restaurant last Saturday evening. We had booked a table for six to celebrate my parents’ anniversary, so we were hoping for a memorable night.

Sadly, the evening did not go as planned. We waited forty minutes for our appetizers, and two main dishes arrived lukewarm. Moreover, our server seemed inattentive and forgot our drink order twice. When I politely raised the issue, a staff member simply shrugged.

As a result, the celebration was ruined. I would like you to look into how your team handled our table. I also expect a partial refund for the meal and a sincere apology from the staff involved. In addition, a complimentary dessert on our next visit would show that you value your customers.

I would be grateful if you could reply by the end of the week. We have been loyal guests for years, and I hope to keep dining with you.

Sincerely,

Maria Lopez

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
appetizers small dishes served before the main course We ordered two appetizers to share.
lukewarm only slightly warm, not hot The soup was lukewarm when it arrived.
inattentive not paying enough attention The inattentive waiter ignored our requests.
refund money given back I asked for a refund for the cold meal.
complimentary given free of charge The hotel offers a complimentary breakfast.

1.3 Unreliable Internet

Your internet service has been unreliable for weeks. Write an email to the provider explaining the issue and requesting compensation.

Sample

Subject: Unreliable Internet Service – Request for Compensation

Dear Customer Support Team,

I am writing to bring to your attention the ongoing problems with my home internet connection (account number 70915). For the past three weeks, the service has been unreliable, and I experience frequent outages every day.

I work remotely, so this has seriously affected my job. I missed an important video meeting last Tuesday because the connection dropped repeatedly. Furthermore, I have called your helpline three times, but the problem has not been resolved. Each technician promised a follow-up visit, yet nobody has come.

Consequently, I would like a technician to visit this week to fix the problem. In addition, I am requesting compensation in the form of a credit on my next bill, since I have paid the full price for a service I have not received.

I would appreciate your prompt attention to this matter. If the issue continues, I will have to consider switching to another provider.

Thank you for your time and assistance.

Yours sincerely,

David Chen

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
unreliable not working consistently My unreliable connection drops every hour.
compensation payment for a loss or inconvenience She received compensation for the delay.
outages periods when a service stops working The storm caused several power outages.
resolved solved or settled The issue was resolved after two days.
follow-up a later action to check or finish something The company never made a follow-up call.
provider a company that supplies a service I am changing my internet provider.

1.4 Double Gym Charge

A gym membership was charged twice. Write an email to customer service asking for a correction.

Sample

Subject: Duplicate Charge on My Membership Account

Dear Customer Service Team,

I am writing to inform you of an error on my gym membership account (member number 55102). When I checked my bank statement this morning, I noticed that I had been charged twice for my monthly fee of $45 on June 1. I have attached a copy of my statement showing both transactions.

I have been a member for two years, and this has never happened before. The second payment left my account almost empty, which caused me some inconvenience.

As this is clearly a billing error, I would like the extra $45 to be reimbursed to my credit card as soon as possible. Additionally, please confirm in writing that my account has been corrected, so that the mistake does not happen again next month.

However, I do not wish to cancel my membership, because I enjoy your facilities and classes. Therefore, I hope we can resolve this matter quickly.

Thank you in advance for your help. Please contact me by email or at 555-0147 if you need more information.

Sincerely,

Julia Martin

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
membership the right to use a club or service My gym membership renews every month.
statement a document listing account activity I checked my bank statement last night.
charged twice billed two times for the same thing I was charged twice for one coffee.
transactions payments or money movements on an account All transactions appear on the statement.
reimbursed paid back The company reimbursed me for the extra fee.

1.5 Late and Wrong Order

Your online order arrived two weeks late and the item was wrong. Write an email to the store asking for a solution.

Sample

Subject: Late Delivery and Wrong Item – Order 83920

Dear Store Manager,

I am writing to express my frustration with my recent online order (number 83920). It was supposed to arrive within five days, but it showed up two weeks late. To make matters worse, the item inside was a blue backpack instead of the black hiking jacket I had ordered.

I needed the jacket for a trip last weekend, so the delay caused me real inconvenience. Moreover, your website still lists the jacket as in stock, which makes me wonder how the mistake happened in your warehouse.

Therefore, I would like you to ship the correct jacket immediately by express delivery at no extra cost. Alternatively, I am happy to accept a full refund once you receive the incorrect item, which I will return in its original packaging. Please send me a prepaid return label.

I trust that you will resolve this matter promptly. I have shopped with you for years, and I hope to continue doing so.

Sincerely,

Karen Walsh

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
inconvenience trouble or difficulty caused to someone The delay was a serious inconvenience.
warehouse a large building where goods are stored The order was packed at a warehouse near the airport.
express delivery faster shipping for an extra fee I chose express delivery to get it by Friday.
refund money given back They offered a full refund.
incorrect item a product that is not the one ordered The incorrect item had to be sent back.
return label a printed tag for sending a parcel back The store emailed me a return label.

1.6 Cancelled Flight

Your flight was cancelled and the airline gave you no help. Write an email to customer service asking for compensation.

Sample

Subject: Cancelled Flight AC 214 – Request for Compensation

Dear Customer Relations Team,

I am writing to request compensation for flight AC 214 from Toronto to Vancouver on August 9, which your airline cancelled only three hours before departure.

When I arrived at the airport, no staff member was available at the service desk, and the airline’s phone line was constantly busy. Furthermore, nobody offered me a meal, a hotel room, or information about alternative flights. I had to rebook a seat for the next afternoon at my own expense and spend the night at the airport.

As a result, I missed an important family event and paid $120 for accommodation and food. I have attached all my receipts.

In light of this, I am asking for a full refund of the cancelled ticket, along with reimbursement of my out-of-pocket expenses. Otherwise, I will have to contact the Canadian Transportation Agency and file a formal complaint about the lack of assistance I received.

I look forward to your prompt response.

Yours sincerely,

Daniel Rossi

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
departure the time a trip begins The departure was delayed by two hours.
rebook to arrange a new reservation The airline helped us rebook our flight.
accommodation a place to stay, such as a hotel We needed accommodation for one night.
reimbursement money paid back for costs I am seeking reimbursement for my meals.
out-of-pocket expenses money you paid yourself Out-of-pocket expenses included taxis and food.
assistance help or support The staff gave us no assistance.

1.7 Misleading Hotel Room

A hotel room did not match its online description. Write an email to the hotel manager explaining the problems and requesting a partial refund.

Sample

Subject: Room Did Not Match Online Description – Booking 4471

Dear Hotel Manager,

I am writing to bring to your attention several problems during my recent stay in room 308 from May 3 to 6.

Your website described a spacious room with an ocean view, a balcony, and a quiet environment. In reality, the room was small, faced the parking lot, and had no balcony. Moreover, construction noise started at 7:00 every morning, and the air conditioner was out of order. I reported these issues to the front desk, but nothing was done.

As a result, I paid for features that I never received. I believe it is only fair that you give me a partial refund, perhaps 40 percent of the total cost of $540. Otherwise, I will have to share my experience in an online review and contact my credit card company about the misleading listing.

I have stayed at your hotel several times and always enjoyed it, so I hope we can settle this matter quickly.

I look forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,

Peter Nguyen

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
spacious having plenty of room We wanted a spacious suite.
out of order not working The elevator was out of order all week.
refund money returned He received a partial refund for the room.
misleading giving a false or unclear idea The photos were misleading.
listing an advertisement or entry online The listing said the room had a view.

1.8 Charged After Cancelling

You were charged for a subscription you cancelled. Write an email asking them to stop the charges and refund you.

Sample

Subject: Charges After Cancelled Subscription – Account 20817

Dear Customer Service Team,

I am writing to dispute the charges on my credit card for a subscription that I cancelled on January 15. I received a confirmation email that day, which I have attached for your reference.

Despite that, I was billed $19.99 on February 15 and again on March 15. I have not used the service since I cancelled, and I did not receive any warning about additional charges.

Therefore, I would like you to stop all future payments immediately and terminate my account. In addition, please refund the two unauthorized charges, which total $39.98, to my original payment method.

I would appreciate it if you could confirm by email once the refund has been processed. Otherwise, I will have no choice but to ask my bank to dispute the transactions.

I have been a customer for three years, and I would like to leave on good terms. Thank you for your understanding.

Sincerely,

Laura Evans

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
dispute to formally challenge a charge I plan to dispute the charge with my bank.
confirmation proof that something is done I received a confirmation email.
billed charged money for a service I was billed twice this month.
terminate to end officially Please terminate my membership.
refund to give money back They will refund the full amount.
unauthorized done without permission There was an unauthorized payment on my card.

1.9 Poor Phone Repair

A repair shop did not fix your phone properly. Write an email to the owner asking them to correct the work.

Sample

Subject: Unresolved Phone Problem After Repair on June 5

Dear Mr. Patel,

I am writing to let you know that the repair work on my phone, completed at your shop on June 5, was not done properly. I paid $120 to replace the battery and fix a faulty charging port.

Unfortunately, the problems are still there. The phone drains its battery within three hours, and the charging cable keeps coming loose. Furthermore, there is now a small scratch near the camera, which was not there when I brought it in.

For this reason, I would like you to inspect the phone again and rectify the work at no additional cost. Moreover, I would appreciate an explanation of what went wrong. If this is not possible, I would expect a refund of the repair fee.

Your shop was recommended by a coworker, so I would like to give you the chance to make this right. I can bring the phone in any weekday after 5:00 p.m., or on the weekend if that suits you better.

I look forward to hearing from you soon.

Sincerely,

Omar Haddad

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
faulty not working correctly The faulty switch had to be replaced.
drains uses up energy quickly The old app drains my battery.
scratch a thin mark on a surface There is a scratch on the glass.
inspect to examine carefully The technician will inspect the screen.
rectify to fix or correct We will rectify the error today.
repair fee the price charged for fixing something The repair fee was higher than expected.

1.10 Course Worse Than Advertised

A course you paid for was much worse than advertised. Write an email to the school asking for a refund.

Sample

Subject: Request for Refund – Business English Course

Dear Admissions Office,

I am writing to request a refund for the Business English course that I completed last month. I paid $850 because your website promised small classes, experienced teachers, and individual feedback.

Unfortunately, the course was far below that standard. The class had more than thirty students, so I never had the chance to speak. Furthermore, the instructor changed three times, and the course materials were outdated. I also never received any written feedback on my assignments.

As a result, I gained very little from the course, even though I attended every session. I feel that the advertised description was misleading, and the price was not justified.

Therefore, I would like a full refund of my tuition. If a full refund is not possible, I would consider a partial refund of at least 70 percent.

I have attached my receipt and attendance record. I would appreciate a response within ten days.

Sincerely,

Nina Petrova

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
feedback comments about your work The teacher gave helpful feedback.
course materials books and handouts for a class The course materials were out of date.
advertised promoted publicly The advertised price was $500.
misleading giving a wrong impression The brochure was misleading.
justified having a good reason The high fee was not justified.
tuition the fee paid for teaching Tuition is due before classes start.

1.11 Pottery Class Schedule Change

You registered for an evening pottery course and the centre moved it to weekday afternoons, when you work. Write an email asking for a solution.

Sample

Subject: Schedule Change for the Evening Pottery Course

Dear Program Coordinator,

I am writing regarding the Evening Pottery course that I registered for on September 2. I was disappointed to learn that the centre has moved the class to weekday afternoons, from 1:00 to 3:00 p.m.

I chose this course specifically because it was offered in the evening. I work full-time until 5:00 p.m., so I cannot attend the new sessions. Moreover, I already paid the full fee of $240, and I was not told about the change when I enrolled.

Under these circumstances, I would like you to consider one of the following options. First, you could offer a second class in the evening for those who cannot attend. Alternatively, you could transfer my registration to a future evening course. If neither option is possible, I would request a full refund.

I have been looking forward to learning this craft, and I hope we can find a workable solution.

Thank you for your understanding. I look forward to your reply.

Sincerely,

Helen Brooks

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
enrolled officially joined a course I enrolled in the class last week.
registration the process of signing up Registration closes on Friday.
refund money returned She asked for a refund of the course fee.
craft a skill of making things by hand Pottery is a relaxing craft.
workable able to be done or used We need a workable plan.

1.12 Disorganized After-School Program

Your child’s after-school program is disorganized. Write an email to the program coordinator describing the problems and suggesting improvements.

Sample

Subject: Concerns About the After-School Program

Dear Ms. Carter,

I am writing to share some concerns about the after-school program that my daughter, Emma, attends on weekdays. She enjoys the activities, but I have noticed several problems in recent weeks.

First, the pickup process is chaotic. Children leave with different adults without any sign-out procedure, which worries me about their safety. In addition, the daily schedule changes without notice, so parents often do not know whether a trip or event is planned. Furthermore, there seem to be too few staff members for the number of children.

To improve the situation, I would suggest a written sign-out sheet and a weekly schedule sent by email. It would also help if you could add one more supervisor during busy hours.

Overall, I believe these small changes would make the program safer and more organized for everyone. I would be happy to volunteer one afternoon a week or meet with you to discuss ideas.

Thank you for taking my concerns seriously.

Sincerely,

Rachel Kim

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
chaotic very disorganized The first day was chaotic.
sign-out a record of who leaves and when Parents must complete a sign-out sheet.
procedure an official way of doing something The safety procedure is simple.
supervisor a person who watches over others One supervisor looks after ten children.
organized well planned and tidy The new system is more organized.
volunteer to offer help without payment I volunteer at the school library.

I have attached a copy of the bill with the charge highlighted. Until this matter is resolved, I will pay only the $85 that I owe. However, I would like to avoid any late fees while you review my request.

Thank you for your prompt attention.

Sincerely,

Anthony Reid

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
invoice a bill listing items and costs The invoice arrived by email.
subscribed signed up to receive a service I never subscribed to that plan.
corrected changed to remove mistakes Please send a corrected statement.
unexplained without a clear reason There was an unexplained fee.
highlighted marked to stand out I highlighted the wrong charge.

1.14 Spoiled Grocery Delivery

Your online grocery delivery arrived with wrong or spoiled items. Write an email to the store describing the problem and asking for a refund or replacement.

Sample

Subject: Wrong and Spoiled Items in Grocery Order 30915

Dear Customer Service Team,

I am writing to report a problem with my grocery delivery, order number 30915, which arrived this morning, November 8.

When I unpacked the bags, I discovered that the milk was past its expiry date and the strawberries were mouldy. In addition, I received a bag of white rice instead of the brown rice I had ordered, and two perishable items, the chicken and the yogurt, were warm because the delivery was left outside.

As a result, I could not use most of the order and had to go shopping again, which was inconvenient with two small children. I have taken photos of the items and attached them to this email.

I would like you to refund the cost of the spoiled and incorrect items, which comes to $31.50. Alternatively, I would accept a replacement delivery if it can arrive before 6:00 p.m. today.

I would also suggest that your drivers confirm receipt of cold products, so that this does not happen to other customers.

Thank you for your help with this matter.

Sincerely,

Fatima Ali

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
spoiled no longer fresh or safe to eat The spoiled meat smelled bad.
expiry date the last safe day to use a product Check the expiry date before eating.
