CELPIP Speaking Task 5: Comparing and Persuading

1 Housing and Places to Live

Topics: apartments, houses, locations, shared housing, renting versus buying.

General Template

I would strongly recommend [your choice]. First of all, [reason 1 with a benefit].

On top of that, [reason 2 or an example]. Admittedly, [the other option has one advantage], but [why yours is still better].

That’s why I really think [your choice] is [the better option for you].

1.1 Condo vs House

Your family is choosing between a condo downtown and a house in the suburbs. Choose one and persuade your family.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the condo downtown. First of all, it’s close to our jobs, schools, and shops, so we’d save hours every week on the commute. That means more family time.

On top of that, a condo needs very little maintenance. There’s no lawn to mow and no snow to shovel, because the building takes care of it. We’d also enjoy amenities like a gym and a pool.

Admittedly, the suburban house has a bigger yard, but we rarely use it, and we could visit a park nearby. Plus, downtown has restaurants, museums, and public transit right outside our door.

That’s why I really think the condo is the better option for our busy lifestyle.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
commute the daily trip to work His commute is short.
maintenance the work of keeping something in good condition The house needs regular maintenance.
amenities extra facilities in a building The amenities include a pool.
public transit buses and trains I take public transit.

1.2 Cheap vs Close Apartment

A friend is picking between two apartments, one cheaper and farther from work, one expensive and close. Persuade them to choose the one you prefer.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the cheaper apartment. First of all, the lower rent will save you a lot of money every month, and you can put that money toward savings or travel.

On top of that, being farther from work isn’t a big problem if there’s a good train or bus line. You could read, listen to podcasts, or relax during the ride. Furthermore, a cheaper place reduces your financial stress, which makes life much more enjoyable.

Admittedly, the expensive apartment is more convenient, but you’d be paying a premium for a few saved minutes, and an unexpected expense could put you under pressure.

That’s why I really think the cheaper apartment is the smarter investment for you.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
financial stress worry about money Rent can cause financial stress.
premium an extra amount paid for something You pay a premium for the view.
investment something that gives long-term value A good education is an investment.

1.3 Living Alone vs Sharing

Your sister is choosing between living alone and sharing an apartment with friends. Persuade her.

Sample

I would strongly recommend sharing an apartment with friends. First of all, you’d split the rent and the bills, so you’d pay much less than you would on your own. That’s a huge help, especially at the start of your career.

On top of that, you’d have company, and you’d never feel lonely. Cooking dinners, watching movies, and chatting after work make life warmer. Also, friends can help each other in emergencies.

Admittedly, living alone gives you more privacy, but you can still enjoy personal space in your own bedroom, and you can agree on house rules to keep things peaceful.

That’s why I really think sharing is the better option. It’s affordable and fun.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
personal space privacy and room to be alone I need some personal space.
house rules agreed guidelines at home Our house rules are simple.
affordable not too expensive The apartment is affordable.

1.4 Renting vs Buying

Your parents are deciding between renting a large place and buying a smaller one. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend buying the smaller place. First of all, when you rent, you pay every month and own nothing. With a purchase, your payments build equity, so you’d own something valuable in the future.

On top of that, you’d have stability. A landlord can raise the rent or sell the building, but if you own your home, you decide how long you’ll stay. Also, property usually appreciates in value over time.

Admittedly, the large rental has more space, but at your age, you don’t need much room, and a smaller home is cheaper to heat and clean.

That’s why I really think buying is the wiser choice for your retirement.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
equity the value of the part of a property you own Equity grows as you pay your mortgage.
stability the state of being steady and secure A home gives stability.
appreciates increases in value The house appreciated by ten percent.
retirement the time of life after working They plan for retirement.

1.5 Town vs City

Your friend has to choose between a quiet town and a lively city. Persuade them to pick one.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the lively city. First of all, a city offers many more job opportunities, and you can find work in almost any field. You’d also meet people from different backgrounds.

On top of that, there’s always something to do, such as concerts, restaurants, festivals, and museums. Besides, public transportation makes it easy to get around without a car.

Admittedly, a quiet town is peaceful, but it can feel isolating, and you’d probably get bored after a few months. In a city, you can always find a vibrant community, and you can escape to nature on weekends.

That’s why I really think the city is the better option for you.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
opportunities chances for success The city has many opportunities.
public transportation buses, trains and subways Public transportation is efficient.
isolating making you feel alone A small village can be isolating.
vibrant full of life The area is vibrant at night.

2 Shopping and Purchases

Topics: choosing a car, a phone, a laptop, furniture, appliances, gifts.

General Template

On top of that, [reason 2 or an example]. Admittedly, [the other option has one advantage], but [why yours is still better].

That’s why I really think [your choice] is [the better option for you].

2.1 Small Car vs SUV

Your family is choosing between a small, fuel-efficient car and a large SUV. Choose one and persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the small, fuel-efficient car. First of all, gas prices are very high, and a small car uses much less fuel, so we’d save hundreds of dollars a year.

On top of that, it’s easier to drive and park in the city, especially on narrow streets. Also, it’s more environmentally friendly, because it produces fewer emissions, and the insurance is usually cheaper.

Admittedly, the SUV has more room, but we only travel with a few people most of the time, and for big trips, we can rent a larger vehicle. A small car is also less expensive to maintain.

That’s why I really think the small car is the more practical choice for our family.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
environmentally friendly not harmful to nature Bikes are environmentally friendly.
maintain keep in good condition It’s costly to maintain an SUV.
practical sensible and useful A practical car saves money.

2.2 Lightweight vs Powerful Laptop

Your friend is picking between two laptops, one lightweight and one with more power. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the lightweight laptop. First of all, it’s much easier to carry around, so you can take it to class, to a café, or on a trip without back pain.

On top of that, the battery life on lightweight models is usually longer, which means you won’t need to look for a power outlet all day. Also, you said you only use it for browsing, writing, and watching videos, so you don’t need extra processing power.

Admittedly, the powerful laptop is faster, but it’s heavy, noisy, and often more expensive, and you’d pay for performance you’ll never use.

That’s why I really think the lightweight laptop is the smarter choice for your lifestyle.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
battery life how long a device works without charging The battery life is twelve hours.
processing power the speed of a computer’s brain Games need more processing power.
smarter wiser A smarter choice saves time.

2.3 Stylish vs Comfortable Sofa

You are helping your parents choose a sofa, one stylish and one comfortable. Persuade them to pick one.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the comfortable sofa. First of all, you’ll sit on it every day, so comfort matters more than looks. A soft sofa helps you relax after a long day and is great for movie nights.

On top of that, it’s better for your health. A supportive sofa is good for your back, and you can even take a nap on it. Also, comfortable sofas are usually made with quality cushions that last for years.

Admittedly, the stylish sofa looks great, but the seat is hard, and guests won’t want to stay long. You can always add colourful pillows and a nice blanket to improve the look.

That’s why I really think the comfortable sofa is the better investment.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
supportive giving good physical support A supportive chair helps my back.
colourful bright and varied in colour A colourful rug brightens the room.
investment something that gives long-term value Good furniture is an investment.

2.4 Jewellery vs Trip

Your brother needs a gift for his wife and is choosing between jewellery and a trip. Persuade him.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the trip. First of all, jewellery is nice, but a trip creates lasting memories that you’ll enjoy together for years. You’ll talk about it long after you come home.

On top of that, spending quality time together will strengthen your relationship. A weekend in a beautiful place, away from work, gives you a chance to relax, and she’ll feel truly valued.

Admittedly, jewellery is something she can wear every day, but she may not like the style you choose, and it can be costly. A trip can be planned according to your budget, and it’s more personal.

That’s why I really think the trip is the better gift. It’s an experience money can’t replace.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
lasting memories memories that stay for a long time The trip left lasting memories.
costly expensive A diamond ring is costly.
budget a plan for spending Stay within your budget.
experience something you live through Travel is a great experience.

2.5 Two Smartphones

Your friend is choosing between two smartphones with different prices and features. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the cheaper smartphone. First of all, it still has all the main features you need, such as a good camera, a fast processor, and enough storage. There’s no reason to pay more.

On top of that, you’d save about three hundred dollars, which you could use for a protective case, headphones, or even a weekend trip. Also, new phones come out every year, so an expensive model loses its value quickly.

Admittedly, the pricier phone has a slightly better screen and some extra features, but you probably won’t notice the difference in daily life, and they’re not worth the extra cost.

That’s why I really think the cheaper phone offers better value for you.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
storage space for saving files I need more storage.
protective case a cover that prevents damage I bought a protective case.
extra cost additional money There’s no extra cost.

3.1 Resort vs Sightseeing

Your family is choosing between a beach resort and a city sightseeing tour for vacation. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the beach resort. First of all, we all work and study hard, so we need a real break. At a resort, we can unwind by the pool, swim in the ocean, and let someone else cook for us.

On top of that, there’s something for everyone. The kids can join the activities, and the adults can enjoy the spa. Also, everything is all-inclusive, so there’s no stress about planning or spending.

Admittedly, a city tour shows us history and culture, but it means walking all day and rushing between attractions, and we’d come home more tired than before.

That’s why I really think the beach resort is the better way to recharge as a family.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
unwind relax I unwind with a book.
all-inclusive with meals and drinks included in the price It’s an all-inclusive resort.
recharge restore energy We need to recharge.

3.2 Hotel vs Rental

Your friends are deciding between a hotel and a vacation rental. Choose one and persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the vacation rental. First of all, with a group of friends, it works out cheaper. We can split the cost, and we’d get a whole house with several bedrooms instead of paying for separate hotel rooms.

On top of that, a rental has a fully equipped kitchen, so we can cook some meals and save money on restaurants. Also, we’d have a living room where we can hang out together, which is much more fun than meeting in a hallway.

Admittedly, a hotel has daily cleaning and a front desk, but it’s less private, and the amenities we’d actually use are all in the rental too.

That’s why I really think the rental gives us more flexibility and better value for money.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
fully equipped having everything needed The kitchen is fully equipped.
amenities useful facilities The rental has great amenities.
value for money good quality for the price The apartment is great value for money.

3.3 Bus Tour vs Independent

Your parents are picking between a bus tour and exploring on their own. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the bus tour. First of all, everything is organized for you. The hotels, the transport, and the sightseeing are all arranged, so you don’t need to worry about directions or timetables.

On top of that, a tour guide will explain the history of every place, and you’ll learn far more than you would with a guidebook. Also, you’ll meet other travellers of your age, and it’s very safe, which is important when visiting a foreign country.

Admittedly, exploring alone gives you more freedom, but it can be stressful, especially with a language barrier, and you could waste time getting lost.

That’s why I really think the bus tour is the more hassle-free and enjoyable option for you.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
directions information about how to get somewhere We asked for directions.
tour guide a person who leads tourists The tour guide was funny.
hassle-free without trouble It was a hassle-free trip.
enjoyable giving pleasure It was an enjoyable week.

3.4 Cabin vs Camping

Your friend is choosing between a mountain cabin and a camping trip. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the mountain cabin. First of all, you’d have a comfortable bed, a roof over your head, and a warm fireplace. After a long hike, that’s exactly what you need.

On top of that, a cabin is more reliable in bad weather. If it rains or gets cold at night, you’ll stay dry and cozy, whereas in a tent, you might be miserable. Also, you can cook in a real kitchen and keep your food fresh.

Admittedly, camping is cheaper and feels more adventurous, but it needs a lot of gear and effort to set up and clean.

That’s why I really think the cabin is the better choice. You’ll enjoy the scenery and peace without the discomfort.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
gear equipment for an activity Camping gear is heavy.
scenery natural beauty The scenery was amazing.
peace calm and quiet I love the peace of the mountains.

3.5 Flying vs Driving

Your family is picking between flying and driving to a destination. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend flying. First of all, it’s much faster. A flight takes two hours, whereas driving would take almost ten, and we’d spend a whole day on the road instead of at our destination.

On top of that, it’s less tiring. We can relax, read, or watch a movie, and nobody has to drive for hours. Also, when you add up the cost of gas, tolls, food, and a hotel stop, the price of the tickets may be comparable.

Admittedly, a car gives us flexibility, but the children will get restless and fussy on a long drive, and there’s the risk of traffic and road construction.

That’s why I really think flying is the more efficient and comfortable choice.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
comparable similar in cost or quality The prices are comparable.
fussy easily annoyed or upset The baby was fussy.
road construction building or repair work on a road Road construction delayed us.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the park. First of all, it’s free, so we can spend the money on food, a cake, and decorations. A restaurant bill for a big group can be very expensive.

On top of that, a park gives us lots of space for games like volleyball or frisbee, and nobody has to sit still at a table. Also, we can bring our own music and have a barbecue, and we won’t have to worry about being too loud.

Admittedly, a restaurant is protected from the weather, but we can check the forecast and book a shelter just in case.

That’s why I really think the park is the better venue. It will be more relaxed, more fun, and more memorable.

4.2 Bowling vs Hike

Your group is choosing a weekend activity: bowling or a hike. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the hike. First of all, spending time in nature is great for our health, and we’d get exercise and fresh air. After a week of work, it’s the best way to clear our heads.

On top of that, hiking gives us time to talk, because we’re walking side by side, while at a bowling alley it’s loud, and we mostly watch the pins. Also, it’s almost free, and the views are spectacular.

Admittedly, bowling is fun and indoors, but we can do it any day of the year, and a hike on a beautiful weekend is a rare chance.

That’s why I really think the hike is the better choice. We’ll come home refreshed and in a positive mood.

4.4 Gym vs Yoga

That’s why I really think yoga is the better option. It’s gentle, and it will fit your lifestyle.

5 Transportation and Commuting

5.1 Driving vs Bus

Your friend is choosing how to get to work: driving or taking the bus. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend taking the bus. First of all, you’d avoid the stress of heavy traffic. Instead of gripping the steering wheel, you could read, answer emails, or listen to music on the way.

On top of that, it’s much cheaper. Gas, insurance, and especially downtown parking fees add up to a lot every month, but a monthly bus pass costs a fraction of that. Also, it’s better for the environment, since you’d reduce your carbon footprint.

Admittedly, a car gives you freedom, but the bus stops right near your office, and you’d never have to search for a spot.

That’s why I really think the bus is the better option. It’s economical and stress-free.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
parking fees money paid to park Parking fees are high downtown.
carbon footprint the amount of carbon you cause to be released Walking reduces your carbon footprint.
economical saving money An economical choice.
stress-free without worry A stress-free commute.

5.2 Electric vs Gas Car

Your brother is deciding between an electric car and a gas car. Persuade him.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the electric car. First of all, it costs far less to run. Electricity is cheaper than gas, and electric cars need almost no maintenance, since there are no oil changes.

On top of that, you’d help the environment by producing no exhaust fumes. Also, the government offers incentives, like tax rebates, which lower the purchase price.

Admittedly, a gas car can be refuelled in minutes, but you could charge an electric car at home overnight, and the number of public chargers is growing quickly.

That’s why I really think the electric car is the better option. It’s sustainable, quiet, and a smart choice for the future.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
maintenance work to keep something in good condition Maintenance is cheap.
exhaust gas released by an engine Exhaust fumes pollute the air.
incentives rewards that encourage you to act There are tax incentives.
sustainable not harming the future Sustainable energy.

5.3 Cycling vs Train

Your friend is choosing between cycling to work and taking the train. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend cycling to work. First of all, you’d get regular exercise without needing a gym membership, and you’ve said that you want to get in shape. Even thirty minutes a day makes a big difference.

On top of that, cycling is free, and you’ll never be stuck waiting for a delayed train. Also, studies show that people who cycle feel happier and more energetic at work.

Admittedly, the train is warmer on cold days, but you can dress properly, and in bad weather, you could take the train just that day. Many offices also have showers and bike parking.

That’s why I really think cycling is the better option. It’s healthy and reliable.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
gym membership a paid right to use a fitness centre A gym membership costs sixty dollars a month.
showers places to wash after exercise The office has showers.
healthy good for your body A healthy habit.

5.4 Train vs Plane

Your family is picking between a train and a plane for a trip. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend the train. First of all, it’s far more relaxing. We don’t need to arrive three hours early, go through security checks, or worry about luggage fees. We just walk in and sit down.

On top of that, the seats are larger, and we can walk around, so the kids won’t get restless. Also, we’ll enjoy scenic views through the window instead of just clouds.

Admittedly, a plane is faster, but when we count the time spent getting to the airport and waiting, the difference is small, and the train takes us right to the city centre.

That’s why I really think the train is the better option. It’s comfortable and fun for the entire family.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
security checks inspections before boarding Security checks take time.
scenic views beautiful landscapes The train offers scenic views.
entire whole The entire family agreed.

5.5 Car vs Taxis and Transit

Your parents are choosing between buying a car and using taxis and public transit. Persuade them.

Sample

I would strongly recommend using taxis and public transit. First of all, owning a car is costly. Besides the purchase price, you’d pay for insurance, gas, repairs, and parking, and the total is surprising.

On top of that, you rarely drive long distances, so a car would sit in the driveway most of the day, losing value. Also, with a transit pass and a ride-sharing app, you can go anywhere without the responsibility of maintenance.

Admittedly, a car is convenient, but you’d also have to deal with traffic and parking problems, and at your age, driving at night can be tiring.

That’s why I really think public options are the better choice. They’re affordable and hassle-free.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence

hassle-free without trouble A hassle-free ride.

6.1 Pay vs Benefits

Your friend is picking between two job offers with different pay and benefits. Persuade them.

Sample
