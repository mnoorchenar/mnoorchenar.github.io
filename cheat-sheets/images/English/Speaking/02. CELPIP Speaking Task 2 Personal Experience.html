CELPIP Speaking Task 2: Personal Experience

1 Memorable Events and Moments

Topics: a special occasion, a celebration, a surprising event, a proud moment, a day you will never forget.

General Template

Let me tell you about [the event]. [It happened when / where / with whom].

At first, [what happened]. Then, [the main moment, with details and feelings].

In the end, [the result].

Looking back, [why it was special or what you learned].

1.1 Memorable Birthday

Talk about a memorable birthday or celebration you have had. What happened and why was it special?

Sample

Let me tell you about my twenty-fifth birthday. It was a day I’ll never forget. I thought my friends had forgotten it, since nobody called me all morning.

At first, I felt a bit sad. However, in the evening my sister asked me to join her for dinner at a small restaurant. When I walked in, the lights came on, and about thirty friends and relatives yelled “Surprise!” There were balloons, a huge cake, and a banner with my name on it.

In the end, we danced and laughed until midnight, and I was overjoyed.

Looking back, it was special because so many people had worked together just to make me feel valued. I’ll treasure that memory forever.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
banner a long sign with words on it They hung a banner above the door.
overjoyed extremely happy She was overjoyed to see her family.
make me feel valued make me feel important Their kind words made me feel valued.
treasure to keep and care about deeply I treasure the photos from that day.

1.2 Feeling Proud

Describe a time when you felt very proud of yourself.

Sample

Let me tell you about the day I finished my first half marathon. A year earlier, I could hardly run for ten minutes, so I decided to challenge myself.

At first, training was exhausting. I woke up at six every morning, and my legs were sore. Then, after a few months, I noticed I was getting stronger. On race day, the weather was cold, and I felt nervous at the start line. Around kilometre eighteen, I wanted to quit, but the crowd was cheering loudly, so I kept going.

In the end, I crossed the finish line in two hours and ten minutes, and tears came to my eyes.

Looking back, I felt a deep sense of accomplishment. It taught me that perseverance can take me further than I expect.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
challenge myself test my own abilities I like to challenge myself with new goals.
cheering shouting to encourage someone The fans were cheering for the runners.
accomplishment something achieved with effort Graduating was a great accomplishment.
perseverance continuing despite difficulty Perseverance helped her pass the exam.

1.3 A Surprise

Talk about a surprise you received. What was it and how did you feel?

Sample

Let me tell you about a surprise I got last spring. I was working late at the office, feeling tired and a little stressed.

At first, I thought it was a normal evening. Then, my manager walked in with a large envelope and said it was for me. Inside, I found a plane ticket to Vancouver and a note from my husband. He had secretly arranged a weekend trip, and my boss had helped by clearing my schedule.

I was completely speechless. My heart was racing, and I couldn’t stop smiling.

In the end, we had a fantastic weekend by the ocean.

Looking back, I was so touched by his effort. It showed me how much he cares about me.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
clearing my schedule removing my other plans to free time He helped by clearing my schedule for Friday.
speechless unable to speak because of strong feelings I was speechless when I saw the gift.
touched emotionally moved She was touched by the kind message.

1.4 Best Day of Your Life

Describe the best day of your life so far.

Sample

Let me tell you about my wedding day. It took place in June, in a small garden near my hometown, and the weather was perfect.

At first, I was extremely nervous, and my hands were shaking while I was getting ready. Then, I saw my future wife walking down the aisle in a beautiful white dress, and all my worries disappeared. We exchanged vows in front of our families, and many people had tears of joy.

After that, we enjoyed a delicious dinner, heartfelt speeches, and dancing under the stars. It was a truly joyful atmosphere.

Looking back, it was the best day of my life because I was surrounded by everyone I love. It was the start of a new chapter, and I’ll cherish every moment.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
exchanged vows made promises to each other in a wedding They exchanged vows by the lake.
joyful full of happiness The joyful crowd clapped and sang.
start of a new chapter the beginning of a new stage in life Moving abroad was the start of a new chapter.
cherish to value highly I cherish my childhood friends.

1.5 Feeling Nervous

Talk about a time when you were very nervous before an event.

Sample

Let me tell you about the time I gave a speech at my best friend’s wedding. I’d never spoken in front of so many people, and I was terrified.

At first, I practised for days, but when I stood up, my mind went blank. My palms were sweaty, and my voice was trembling. I could see over a hundred faces looking at me.

Then, I took a deep breath, looked at my friend, and started with a funny story about our university days. Everyone laughed, and I relaxed immediately.

In the end, the speech went well, and people thanked me afterwards.

Looking back, I learned that being nervous is normal, and a genuine story is better than a perfect speech. I felt a great sense of relief.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
trembling shaking slightly His hands were trembling with fear.
genuine honest and real She gave a genuine smile.
sense of relief the feeling that a worry has gone I felt a sense of relief when it ended.

2 Trips, Places, and Travel

Topics: a holiday, a memorable trip, a place you visited, a getaway, getting lost.

General Template

Let me tell you about [the trip or place]. [It happened when / where / with whom].

At first, [arrival or first impression]. Then, [the main activity or event, with details and feelings].

In the end, [the result].

Looking back, [why it was special or what you learned].

2.1 Memorable Trip

Talk about a memorable trip you have taken. Where did you go and what did you do?

Sample

Let me tell you about my trip to Banff National Park two summers ago. I went with three close friends, and we drove from Calgary.

At first, I was amazed by the breathtaking mountains and the turquoise lakes. Then, we spent three days hiking, canoeing, and camping under the stars. One morning, we even saw a bear from a safe distance, which was both exciting and scary.

After that, we relaxed in the hot springs, and our tired muscles felt wonderful.

Looking back, it was memorable because I was totally disconnected from the city life. I came home feeling refreshed, and I’m already dreaming of going back.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
breathtaking extremely beautiful The view from the top was breathtaking.
disconnected from separated from I felt disconnected from my daily stress.
refreshed full of energy again I woke up refreshed after the trip.
hot springs natural pools of warm water We soaked in the hot springs.

2.2 A Place to Revisit

Describe a place that you visited and would like to go back to.

Sample

Let me tell you about Lisbon, the capital of Portugal. I spent a week there last autumn, and I fell in love with it.

At first, I loved the colourful buildings and the narrow cobblestone streets. Then, I discovered the local food. I tried fresh grilled sardines and the famous custard tarts, which were absolutely delicious. In the evenings, I listened to live music in small bars, and the people were incredibly welcoming.

In the end, I didn’t want to leave.

Looking back, I’d love to return because I only saw a small part of the city. I’d like to explore the nearby beaches and soak up the laid-back atmosphere again.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
welcoming friendly to guests The hosts were warm and welcoming.
soak up enjoy and absorb I soaked up the sunshine on the beach.
laid-back relaxed and easygoing It’s a laid-back town with slow mornings.

2.3 Unexpected Travel Problem

Talk about a time when something unexpected happened while you were travelling.

Sample

Let me tell you about the time I missed my connecting flight in Frankfurt. I was travelling to Toronto to see my cousin, and my first plane was delayed by an hour.

At first, I panicked when I saw the departure board. My next flight had already taken off, and the airport was crowded. Then, I went to the service desk, where a friendly agent rebooked me on a flight the next morning and gave me a hotel voucher.

As a result, I spent an evening exploring the old town, which I’d never planned to see.

Looking back, it turned out to be a blessing in disguise. I learned to stay calm and make the best of unexpected situations.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
taken off left the ground, for a plane The plane had already taken off.
rebooked arranged a new reservation They rebooked us on the evening train.
blessing in disguise something that seems bad but turns out good The delay was a blessing in disguise.
make the best of do as well as possible in a bad situation Let’s make the best of the rain.

2.4 Day Trip

Describe a day trip that you enjoyed with friends or family.

Sample

Let me tell you about a day trip my family took to a small lake last July. We left early in the morning and packed sandwiches, fruit, and plenty of water.

At first, the kids were sleepy in the car, but they woke up as soon as we arrived. Then, we rented a paddle boat and spent two hours on the water. After that, we had a picnic under a big tree, and my dad grilled corn on a small portable stove. The kids splashed around in the shallow water until sunset.

In the end, everyone was tired but happy.

Looking back, it was a simple day, but we really bonded as a family, and I’ll always remember the peaceful scenery.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
splashed moved around in water noisily The children splashed in the pool.
bonded formed a close connection The team bonded during the trip.
portable stove a small cooking device that is easy to carry We boiled water on a portable stove.

2.5 Arriving in a New City

Talk about the first time you visited a new city or country.

Sample

Let me tell you about the first time I arrived in Canada, three years ago. I landed in Toronto in the middle of winter, and it was minus fifteen degrees, which was a shock for me.

At first, I felt lost and a little scared. Everything was different, from the language to the public transit system. Then, a stranger at the subway station noticed my confused face and kindly helped me with my ticket and directions.

After that, I walked around downtown and was impressed by the diversity of the people and the food.

Looking back, that day was both difficult and exciting. It was the moment I started to adapt to a new life, and I’m very grateful for it.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
public transit buses, trains and subways for everyone I take public transit to work.
diversity a variety of different people or things Toronto is known for its diversity.
adapt adjust to new conditions It took time to adapt to the climate.

3 People and Relationships

Topics: a person who influenced you, a friend, a teacher, a family member, meeting someone new.

General Template

At first, [background or first impression]. Then, [the main moment or what the person did, with details and feelings].

In the end, [the result].

3.1 Influential Person

Talk about a person who has had a big influence on your life.

Sample

Let me tell you about my grandmother, who has had the biggest influence on my life. She raised five children on a small farm, and she never complained.

At first, I didn’t understand why she worked so hard. Then, as I grew up, I realized that her determination and her kindness were her greatest strengths. She always told me, “Be honest and never give up,” and she lived by those words. When I struggled at school, she sat with me and encouraged me every evening.

In the end, I graduated with honours, and she was the proudest person in the room.

Looking back, she taught me the value of hard work and humility. She’s my role model, and I want to be like her.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
determination firm decision to keep trying Her determination inspired us all.
humility not thinking you are better than others He showed humility after winning.
role model a person others want to copy My coach is a role model for me.

3.2 Help From a Family Member

Describe a time when a friend or family member helped you.

Sample

Let me tell you about the time my older brother helped me when I lost my job. It happened two years ago, and I was feeling hopeless.

At first, I didn’t want to tell anyone, because I was embarrassed. Then, my brother noticed that I was down and asked me what was wrong. After I explained, he invited me to stay at his place for a month, helped me update my résumé, and even introduced me to a friend who was hiring.

In the end, I got a new job within six weeks.

Looking back, I’m deeply grateful for his support. He showed me that family will always have your back when times are tough.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
down sad or discouraged She felt down after the news.
grateful thankful I’m grateful for your help.
have your back protect and help you True friends have your back.

3.3 Memorable Teacher

Talk about a teacher or mentor you remember well.

Sample

Let me tell you about Mr. Hassan, my high school math teacher. I was struggling with algebra, and I was convinced that I was simply bad at math.

At first, I was afraid to ask questions. Then, Mr. Hassan noticed my frustration and offered to give me extra help after class. He explained each concept in a patient way, using real-life examples, and he never made me feel stupid. He also motivated me by praising even my small improvements.

In the end, I raised my grade from forty to eighty-five percent.

Looking back, he taught me that anyone can learn with the right guidance. He inspired me to become a teacher myself.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
patient calm when things take time A patient teacher explains things again.
motivated encouraged to want to do something His words motivated me to study.
inspired filled with the desire to do something She inspired me to travel.

3.4 Meeting a Friend

Describe how you met a good friend.

Sample

Let me tell you about how I met my best friend, Lena. It was the first day of university, and I knew nobody on campus.

At first, I sat alone in the lecture hall, feeling a little out of place. Then, a girl next to me asked if she could borrow a pen, and we started chatting. We discovered that we both loved hiking and the same TV series, so we kept talking until the class ended.

After that, we had lunch together every day, and we hit it off immediately.

Looking back, a simple pen changed my life. Ten years later, we’re still inseparable, and I’m so happy that I struck up a conversation that day.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
out of place uncomfortable in a setting I felt out of place at the party.
hit it off quickly become friends We hit it off at the first meeting.
inseparable always together The two sisters are inseparable.
struck up a conversation started talking He struck up a conversation on the bus.

3.5 Helping Someone

Talk about a time when you helped someone else.

Sample

Let me tell you about the time I helped an elderly neighbour with her groceries. It happened last winter after a heavy snowstorm.

At first, I saw her struggling to carry bags on an icy sidewalk. Then, I rushed over, took the bags, and walked her to her door. When we got inside, I noticed that her heating was broken, so I called a repair service and waited until a technician arrived. I also brought her a hot meal from my kitchen.

In the end, she thanked me with tears in her eyes and gave me a heartfelt hug.

Looking back, it was a small act of kindness, but it made me feel fulfilled. I realized how much a little generosity can matter.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
heartfelt sincere and deeply felt She sent a heartfelt thank-you note.
fulfilled satisfied and happy about your life Volunteering makes me feel fulfilled.
generosity willingness to give to others Their generosity amazed us.

4 Work, School, and Achievements

Topics: a first day, a challenge, an accomplishment, a project, learning something difficult.

General Template

Let me tell you about [the experience]. [It happened when / where / with whom].

At first, [the situation or the difficulty]. Then, [what you did, with details and feelings].

In the end, [the result].

Looking back, [what you learned or why it mattered].

4.1 First Day

Talk about your first day at school or work. What was it like?

Sample

Let me tell you about my first day at my current job as a graphic designer. I was both excited and anxious, so I arrived thirty minutes early.

At first, I felt lost. The office was big, and I didn’t know anybody. Then, my manager introduced me to the team, and everyone gave me a warm welcome. A colleague showed me around, explained the software, and invited me to lunch. In the afternoon, I attended a short orientation and got my first small task.

In the end, I went home exhausted but very happy.

Looking back, I realized that most of my fears were unnecessary. The team was supportive, and I quickly started to feel at home.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
orientation an introduction for new people The orientation lasted two hours.
supportive helpful and encouraging My supportive boss answered every question.
at home comfortable and relaxed in a place I finally felt at home in the new office.

4.2 Overcoming a Challenge

Describe a challenge you overcame at work or school.

Sample

Let me tell you about a tight deadline I faced at work last year. My manager asked me to finish a major report in just three days, and I normally need a week.

At first, I panicked and thought it was impossible. Then, I made a plan. I broke the project into small tasks, asked a colleague to help with the data, and worked late for two evenings. I also turned off my phone to stay focused.

In the end, I submitted the report on time, and my manager praised the quality.

Looking back, I learned the importance of time management and teamwork. Now I feel more confident about handling pressure.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
tight deadline very little time to finish something We had a tight deadline for the launch.
stay focused keep your attention on a task It’s hard to stay focused in a noisy room.
time management the skill of using time well Good time management reduces stress.
confident sure of your abilities He feels confident before exams.

4.3 Learning a New Skill

Talk about a time you learned a new skill. How did you learn it?

Sample

Let me tell you about how I learned to play the guitar. I was thirty, and I had always wanted to play but never had time.

At first, I watched free tutorials online. My fingers hurt, and my chords sounded terrible. Then, I signed up for weekly lessons with a local teacher, who corrected my mistakes and gave me simple songs to practise. I practised daily for twenty minutes, even when I was tired.

After a few months, I could play my favourite songs, and I performed for my family on New Year’s Eve.

Looking back, it taught me that consistency matters more than talent. Learning something new also boosted my self-esteem.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
practised daily did something every day to improve I practised daily before the recital.
consistency doing something regularly Consistency is the key to progress.
boosted my self-esteem made me feel better about myself Winning the prize boosted my self-esteem.

4.4 Proud Project

Describe a project you completed that you are proud of.

Sample

Let me tell you about a community garden project that I led at my university. We wanted to turn an empty lot behind the library into a green space.

At first, we needed permission and funding, so I wrote a proposal and presented it to the student council. Then, we recruited about twenty volunteers. On weekends, we cleared the ground, built wooden planters, and planted vegetables and flowers. It was hard work, but we had a lot of fun.

In the end, the garden opened in May, and students could take fresh produce for free.

Looking back, I’m proud of how we turned an idea into reality. It taught me leadership and how to collaborate with different people.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
recruited found people and asked them to join The club recruited new members in September.
leadership the ability to guide a group She showed strong leadership.
collaborate work together We collaborated on the final report.

4.5 Learning From a Mistake

Talk about a time when you made a mistake and learned from it.

Sample

Let me tell you about a mistake I made during my first month at a restaurant. I was a new waiter, and I was trying to work quickly.

At first, everything seemed fine. Then, I brought a dish with peanuts to a customer who had told me about his allergy. Luckily, he noticed it before eating, but I was mortified. I apologized immediately, and the chef quickly prepared a new meal.

In the end, my manager didn’t scold me. Instead, she explained the importance of double-checking orders.

Looking back, that mistake taught me to be more careful and to take responsibility. Now I always confirm allergies with the kitchen.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
mortified extremely embarrassed I was mortified when I forgot his name.
double-checking checking something a second time Double-checking prevents errors.
responsibility a duty you are accountable for She takes responsibility for her mistakes.
allergies bad reactions to certain foods or substances He has allergies to nuts.

5 Childhood, Traditions, and Memories

Topics: a childhood memory, a family tradition, a favourite holiday, a childhood place, growing up.

General Template

At first, [the setting or the beginning]. Then, [the main moment, with details and feelings].

5.1 Childhood Memory

Talk about a favourite childhood memory.

Sample

Let me tell you about the summers I spent at my grandparents’ farm. Every July, my parents drove me there, and I stayed for two weeks.

At first, I would run straight to the barn to see the animals. Then, I helped my grandfather feed the chickens and collect eggs. In the afternoons, my cousins and I climbed trees, swam in the river, and picked fresh strawberries. At night, my grandmother told us bedtime stories by the fireplace.

In the end, I always cried when it was time to go home.

Looking back, those days were carefree and full of laughter. I feel nostalgic whenever I smell fresh hay.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
climbed trees went up trees using hands and feet We climbed trees after school.
bedtime stories stories told before sleeping My mother read bedtime stories every night.
carefree without worries We had a carefree childhood.
nostalgic feeling warm sadness about the past Old songs make me nostalgic.

5.2 Family Tradition

Describe a family tradition or holiday you enjoyed as a child.

Sample

Let me tell you about the Eid celebration my family held every year. It started early in the morning, when we put on new clothes and went to the mosque for prayers.

At first, the house was very busy, because my mother and aunts had been cooking for days. Then, relatives from all over the city arrived, and the table was full of sweets and traditional dishes. The children received small gifts of money and ran around the garden, playing games.

In the end, we sat together and shared stories until late at night.

Looking back, this tradition taught me the value of togetherness. I still keep it alive with my own kids.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
tradition a custom passed down in a family or culture Baking cookies is a family tradition.
togetherness the feeling of being close to others The holiday is about togetherness.
keep it alive continue a custom so it does not disappear We promised to keep it alive for our children.

5.3 Childhood Place

Talk about a place from your childhood that you remember well.

Sample

Let me tell you about the small park near my childhood home. It had a big oak tree, a rusty swing set, and a sandy football field.

At first, I only went there with my mother. Then, when I turned eight, I started meeting my friends there every afternoon after school. We played football until it got dark and shared ice cream from a little cart by the gate. In autumn, we collected fallen leaves and made piles to jump into.

In the end, the park became the centre of my social life.

Looking back, it was a place full of innocence and friendship. When I visit my hometown, I always walk through it and reminisce.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
oak tree a large tree with acorns We sat under the old oak tree.
fallen leaves leaves that have dropped from trees The path was covered with fallen leaves.
innocence the simple, pure feelings of childhood I miss the innocence of those years.
reminisce think or talk about happy past times We reminisce about our school days.

5.4 Childhood Toy

Describe a game, toy, or activity you loved as a child.

Sample

Let me tell you about my favourite childhood toy, a set of wooden building blocks. My uncle gave them to me for my fifth birthday.

At first, I only stacked them into towers. Then, I began to create castles, bridges, and even a whole city on the living room floor. My younger sister joined me, and we invented stories about the people who lived there. We would play for hours without getting bored, and my mother had to call us twice for dinner.

In the end, I kept the blocks until I was a teenager.

Looking back, they developed my imagination and my love for design. Today, I work as an architect, so they sparked my career.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
getting bored losing interest I never got bored of that game.
imagination the ability to form ideas in the mind Children have wild imaginations.
sparked started or caused The trip sparked my interest in history.

5.5 Time With a Grandparent

Talk about a time you spent with a grandparent or older relative.

Sample

Let me tell you about an afternoon I spent with my grandfather when I was twelve. He took me fishing at a quiet lake, which was one of his favourite pastimes.

At first, I was bored, because we sat silently for an hour. Then, he began telling me stories about his youth, about growing up in a small village and walking five kilometres to school. I was fascinated. Suddenly, my line moved, and with his help, I caught my first fish.

In the end, we cooked it over a small fire and ate it together.

Looking back, that day made me appreciate his wisdom and patience. It’s a cherished memory, especially since he passed away.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
pastimes activities done for fun Reading is her favourite pastime.
fascinated very interested I was fascinated by his stories.
wisdom knowledge gained from experience The elders shared their wisdom.
cherished loved and valued She keeps a cherished photo of him.

6.1 Meaningful Gift

Talk about a gift you gave or received that meant a lot.
