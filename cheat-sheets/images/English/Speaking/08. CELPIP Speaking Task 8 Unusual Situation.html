CELPIP Speaking Task 8: Unusual Situation

1 Unusual Items and Products

Topics: odd furniture in a store, an unusual bicycle, a strange piece of clothing, a quirky rental, an odd gadget.

General Template

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. [Where you are and what it is].

It looks like [shape, colour, size, material]. On top of that, [one more strange detail].

That’s why [why it is unusual or why you like it]. What do you think? [Your question or request].

1.1 Watermelon Desk

You are in a furniture store and see a desk you want to buy, but the clerk will not let you take a photo. The desk is shaped like a slice of watermelon with banana-shaped legs. Phone a family member and describe it.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. First of all, I’m in a furniture store, and I found a desk I really want to buy, but they won’t let me take a photo, so I need to describe it.

It looks like a giant slice of watermelon. The top is semicircular, with a bright green edge, a red surface, and little black seeds painted on it. On top of that, the legs are shaped like yellow bananas, and they curve slightly outward, which makes the desk look quirky.

That’s why I think it would be fun for my home office. It’s sturdy, and there’s plenty of space for my laptop. What do you think? Should I buy it, or is it too wacky?

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
semicircular shaped like half a circle A semicircular table.
quirky unusual in a charming way A quirky lamp.
sturdy strong and solid A sturdy desk.
wacky funny and strange A wacky design.

1.2 Branch Bicycle

You see an unusual bicycle made entirely of tree branches at a market. Phone a friend and describe it so they can picture it.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. I’m at the weekend market, and there’s a bicycle made entirely of tree branches.

It looks like a normal bike in size, but the frame is made of curved brown branches tied together with rope. On top of that, the wheels are made of thin twigs, bent into circles, and the seat is covered with green moss. Meanwhile, there are even small flowers woven into the handlebars.

That’s why everyone is stopping to take photos. It’s more of an art piece than a vehicle, and I doubt it can really be ridden. What do you think? Do you want me to find out the price for you, as a decoration for your garden?

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
woven made by passing things over and under each other A woven basket.
art piece a work of art An art piece in the gallery.
decoration something that makes a place look nicer A garden decoration.

1.3 Teapot Cabin

You are renting a holiday cabin that looks like a giant teapot. Phone your family and describe what it looks like.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. We’ve just arrived at the holiday cabin, and it looks like a giant teapot!

It looks like a huge white teapot with blue flowers painted on it. The round body is the main room, and the door is in the middle of it. On top of that, there’s a long spout that sticks out on the right, and it’s actually a small balcony. Meanwhile, the lid on top is a skylight with a little golden handle.

That’s why it feels like living in a fairy tale. Inside, the rooms are surprisingly cozy. What do you think? Would you like to come and visit us this weekend?

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
spout the narrow part of a teapot that you pour from The spout was long.
skylight a window in a roof The skylight lets in sun.
cozy warm and comfortable A cozy cabin.

1.4 Animal Jacket

You are in a clothing store and see a jacket with a different animal on each sleeve. Call a friend and describe it so they can decide if you should buy it.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. First of all, I’m in a clothing store, and I found a really weird jacket.

It looks like a regular denim jacket, but each sleeve has a different animal. On the left sleeve, there’s an embroidered green frog, and on the right, there’s an orange fox with a big fluffy tail. On top of that, the back has a large purple whale jumping out of the waves, and the buttons are shaped like little fish.

That’s why it’s very eye-catching, though maybe too colourful for work. It costs eighty dollars. What do you think? Do you think it’s stylish or just over the top?

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
embroidered decorated with stitched designs An embroidered flower.
eye-catching attracting attention An eye-catching dress.
stylish fashionable A stylish coat.
over the top too extreme The design is over the top.

1.5 Hand Lamp

You find an unusual lamp shaped like a hand holding a light bulb. Call your sister and describe it before you buy it.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. First of all, I’m at a little gift shop, and I found the most unusual lamp.

It looks like a human hand, made of shiny black metal, coming out of a round wooden base. The fingers are slightly open and hold a big, round light bulb, as if the hand is offering it to you. On top of that, there’s a long thin wire that goes down the wrist, and the lamp turns on with a small switch in the palm.

That’s why I think it’s a perfect conversation starter for my living room. It’s a bit creepy, but also artistic. What do you think? Should I get it, or look for something more conventional?

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
conversation starter something that makes people talk A conversation starter.
creepy strange and a little scary A creepy doll.
artistic showing creative skill An artistic design.
conventional ordinary and traditional A conventional lamp.

2 Strange Objects in Everyday Places

Topics: an odd item in a room, a strange piece of furniture, a mix-up in a shop, something in the wrong place.

General Template

You won’t believe what I’m looking at. [Where you are and what is odd].

It looks like [details of the object and its surroundings]. On top of that, [one more strange detail].

That’s why [why it is unusual]. I wonder [a question about the reason].

2.1 Living Room Bathtub

A bathtub placed in the middle of a living room next to a television. Describe the unusual situation to a friend over the phone.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. I’m at a friend’s apartment, and there’s a bathtub in the middle of the living room!

It looks like a classic white tub with golden feet, and it’s right in front of a big television. On top of that, there’s a bright pink cushion inside, and a small table with popcorn next to it, so it seems like people actually sit in it to watch movies. Meanwhile, the floor around it is a normal wooden floor, so there’s no plumbing at all.

That’s why it’s so bizarre. I wonder whether it’s a joke or a new kind of sofa. It looks very comfortable!

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
cushion a soft pillow A soft cushion.
plumbing the pipes that carry water No plumbing.
bizarre very strange A bizarre idea.

2.2 Tree Through the Roof

A tree growing through the roof of a house. Describe what you see to someone who cannot see it.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. First of all, I’m standing in front of a small house on a quiet street, and there’s a tree growing right through its roof.

It looks like a tall oak with a thick brown trunk that comes out of the middle of a red tile roof. The roof has a perfect round hole around it, and the branches spread over the house, covering it with green leaves like a giant umbrella. On top of that, there’s a small bird’s nest in the branches, and a ladder leans against the trunk.

That’s why it looks as if the house was built around the tree on purpose. I wonder how the tree stays healthy indoors. It’s truly a remarkable sight.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
healthy in good condition A healthy plant.
remarkable worth noticing A remarkable sight.
ladder a set of steps used to climb up high He climbed the ladder to the roof.

2.3 Shoes on the Ceiling

A shop window displaying shoes on the ceiling. Describe it so that your friend can picture it.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. First of all, I’m walking past a shoe store downtown, and the display in the window is completely upside down.

It looks like all the shoes are attached to the ceiling, as if gravity doesn’t work! There are red high heels, white sneakers, and brown boots, all standing upside down, with their soles pointing up. On top of that, the mannequin is sitting on the ceiling too, and the price tags hang upward.

That’s why everyone on the sidewalk is stopping to stare. It’s a very creative marketing idea, and it certainly grabs attention. I wonder what they’ll do next week. Do you want to come and see it?

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
creative showing imagination A creative display.
marketing promoting products Smart marketing.
grabs attention makes people notice It grabs attention.

2.4 Bike on a Car Roof

A bicycle parked on top of a car roof in a parking lot. Describe the situation.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. I’m in the parking lot of the supermarket, and there’s a bicycle on the roof of a car.

It looks like a small silver car, and a blue mountain bike is sitting on top of it, just lying flat on the roof, without any rack or straps. On top of that, the front wheel is hanging over the windshield, and a helmet is placed neatly on the seat. Meanwhile, there’s nobody around.

That’s why I’m quite puzzled. I wonder whether the owner forgot it there and is about to drive away, which would be a disaster. I might leave a note or tell the security guard.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
puzzled confused I was puzzled.
note a short written message Leave a note.
security guard a person who protects a place The security guard helped.

2.5 Giant Banana Statue

A giant statue of a banana in the middle of a town square. Explain what you see.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. First of all, I’m in the town square, and there’s a gigantic statue of a banana in the middle of it.

It looks like a bright yellow banana, about four metres tall, standing upright on a stone base. It’s glossy, and one side is peeled open, showing a white inside. On top of that, children are climbing the steps around it, and a few tourists are taking selfies in front of it.

That’s why the square feels so cheerful, although it’s totally out of place next to the old stone church. I wonder who decided to build it. There’s a small plaque that says it celebrates the local fruit market.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
glossy smooth and shiny A glossy finish.
selfies photos you take of yourself They took selfies.
plaque a flat sign with writing A plaque on the wall.

3 Odd or Unexpected Events in Public

Topics: a strange parade, unusual clothing, an animal in a strange place, a surprising performance.

General Template

You won’t believe what I’m looking at. [Where you are and what is happening].

It looks like [people, animals, clothing, actions]. On top of that, [one more strange detail].

That’s why [why it is unusual]. I wonder [a question about the reason].

3.1 Dinosaur Costume on the Subway

A man in a full costume reading a newspaper on a crowded subway. Describe the situation.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. I’m on the subway, and there’s a man dressed in a full dinosaur costume sitting across from me.

It looks like a big green T. rex with a long tail, tiny arms, and a huge head with sharp white teeth. On top of that, he’s calmly reading a newspaper, as if it’s completely normal, and he’s holding it with his tiny paws. Meanwhile, the other passengers are trying not to laugh, and a child is staring at him.

That’s why the whole subway car is in a good mood. I wonder whether he’s going to a party or a photo shoot. It’s the most hilarious thing I’ve seen on my commute.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
photo shoot a session for taking photographs A photo shoot downtown.
hilarious very funny A hilarious scene.
commute the daily journey to work My commute is long.

3.2 Sheep on a City Street

A flock of sheep walking down a busy city street. Describe what you see.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. I’m downtown during rush hour, and a flock of sheep is walking down the middle of the street!

It looks like about thirty white sheep, with thick woolly coats, calmly trotting between the cars. On top of that, a farmer with a long stick is walking behind them, and a dog is running along the side, keeping the group together. Meanwhile, the traffic is completely at a standstill, and drivers are filming with their phones.

That’s why the whole street feels like a country road. I wonder where they’re going. It might be a traditional event, or maybe they’ve just wandered off from a farm. Either way, it’s priceless.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
at a standstill not moving Traffic was at a standstill.
wandered off moved away without a plan The sheep wandered off.
priceless extremely valuable or funny A priceless sight.

3.3 Unicycle Juggler

A person riding a unicycle while juggling in a shopping mall. Describe what is happening.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. I’m at the shopping mall, and there’s a person riding a unicycle in the middle of the hall.

It looks like a young man in a striped red and white shirt, balancing on a single wheel, and he’s juggling five colourful balls at the same time. On top of that, he’s wearing a funny hat, and he smiles at the crowd, which has formed a circle around him. Meanwhile, a small girl is clapping, and shoppers are stopping to watch.

That’s why the whole mall is buzzing. I wonder if he’s a professional performer. His coordination is amazing, and he’s definitely getting some tips in his hat.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
juggling keeping several objects in the air He’s juggling three balls.
performer a person who entertains A street performer.
coordination the ability to move body parts smoothly Great coordination.

3.4 Cow in a Parking Lot

A cow standing in a parking lot in front of a supermarket. Describe it to a friend.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. I’m outside the supermarket, and there’s a cow standing in the parking lot!

It looks like a big brown and white cow, calmly chewing something, and it’s standing right between two cars. On top of that, it’s wearing a bell around its neck, so it probably belongs to a farm nearby. Meanwhile, a few customers are keeping a safe distance, and a shopping cart is next to it, as if the cow were waiting in line.

That’s why everyone is smiling and taking pictures. I wonder how it ended up here, and whether someone has called the owner. The store manager is on the phone now, so I think they’re dealing with it.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
shopping cart a basket on wheels for groceries An empty shopping cart.
owner the person who has something The owner arrived.
on the phone talking by phone He’s on the phone.

3.5 Living Statue

A street performer painted to look like a statue, surrounded by people taking selfies. Describe the scene.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. I’m in the main square, and there’s a street performer who looks exactly like a statue.

It looks like a man covered from head to toe in silver paint, standing perfectly still on a small box. He’s wearing a long silver coat and holding a fake book, and he doesn’t even blink. On top of that, a crowd has gathered around him, and many people are taking selfies. Meanwhile, every time someone drops a coin in his bucket, he moves slowly and bows.

That’s why the kids are completely amazed. I wonder how he can stay so still for hours. It takes great patience and discipline.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
fake not real A fake flower.
amazed very surprised The kids were amazed.
patience the ability to wait calmly It takes patience.
discipline self-control Great discipline.

4 Lost, Damaged, or Misdelivered Items

Topics: a lost bag, a damaged parcel, a wrong delivery, a missing pet, a dented car.

General Template

Hello, I’m calling about [the item or problem]. [When and where it happened].

It looks like [size, colour, shape, brand, distinctive features]. On top of that, [contents or extra details].

That’s why [what you need]. Could you please [your request]?

4.1 Bag Left on a Train

You left your bag on a train. Call the lost-and-found and describe the bag and its contents.

Sample

Hello, I’m calling about a bag that I left on the 5:40 train from Ottawa this evening, in the third car.

It looks like a medium-sized black leather backpack, with a silver zipper and a small red ribbon tied to the handle. There’s also a blue luggage tag with my name on it. On top of that, inside, there’s a grey laptop, a brown wallet, a water bottle, and a notebook with a green cover.

That’s why it’s urgent for me to get it back as soon as possible. Could you please check whether someone has handed it in? If so, I can come to the station right away to claim it, and I’ll bring my ID.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
luggage tag a label with the owner’s name A luggage tag on my suitcase.
water bottle a container for drinking water A metal water bottle.
handed it in given to someone in charge The wallet was handed in.

4.2 Damaged Parcel

A parcel arrived with the item badly damaged. Call the store and describe what you received.

Sample

Hello, I’m calling about an order that I received this morning, number 78452.

It looks like the box was crushed on one side, and the packaging is torn. Inside, the ceramic vase I ordered is broken into at least five pieces, and the pieces are scattered around the foam. On top of that, there’s no protective wrapping around it, only a thin sheet of paper, so I think it was poorly packed.

That’s why I’d like a solution. Could you please send a replacement, or issue a refund? In addition, I have taken photos of the box and the broken vase, and I can email them to you right away as proof.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
protective wrapping material that prevents damage Use protective wrapping.
poorly packed not packed with enough care The box was poorly packed.
issue a refund give money back Please issue a refund.

4.3 Wrong Sofa Delivered

You ordered a plain brown sofa but received a bright orange one with stripes. Call customer service and describe it.

Sample

Hello, I’m calling about a sofa that was delivered to my home yesterday.

It looks like a very bright orange sofa with thick white stripes, and it has round cushions in purple. I ordered a plain brown three-seater with square legs, so it’s totally different. On top of that, it’s larger than the one I ordered, and it doesn’t fit through the living-room door, which is why it’s still in the hallway.

That’s why I think there was a mix-up with another customer’s order. Could you please arrange to collect this one and deliver the correct sofa? In addition, I’d like confirmation of the delivery date, and my order number is 5521.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
cushions soft pillows Cushions on the sofa.
mix-up a confusion or mistake A mix-up in the order.
delivery date the day goods arrive Confirm the delivery date.

4.4 Missing Dog

Your dog is missing. Call a neighbour and describe what the dog looks like and where you last saw it.

Sample

Hi, it’s your neighbour from number twelve. I’m calling about my dog, Buddy, who is missing.

It looks like a medium-sized golden retriever with soft, light-brown fur, floppy ears, and a white patch on his chest. On top of that, he’s wearing a red collar with a silver tag, and he limps slightly on his left back leg. In addition, I last saw him around four o’clock, running toward the park behind your house, after the gate was left open.

That’s why I’m asking everyone nearby. Could you please check your garden and garage? If you see him, please call me at once. He’s very friendly, so he’ll come if you call his name. I’d be truly grateful.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
collar a band around a pet’s neck A leather collar.
friendly kind and sociable A friendly dog.
truly grateful very thankful I’d be truly grateful.

4.5 Hit Parked Car

Someone hit your parked car and left a large dent. Call your insurance company and describe the damage.

Sample

Hello, I’m calling about damage to my car that happened last night while it was parked outside my home.

It looks like a large dent, about thirty centimetres wide, on the rear driver’s side door, and the paint is scratched, with some white marks from the other vehicle. On top of that, the rear light is cracked, and a small piece of plastic is lying on the ground. In addition, the other driver didn’t leave a note or any contact details.

That’s why I’d like to make a claim. Could you please explain the steps and what documents I need? I have photos of the damage and a police report number, and my policy number is 90817.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
dent a hollow made by a hit A dent in the door.
claim a request for insurance payment I made a claim.
police report an official record made by police File a police report.

5 Unusual Buildings, Vehicles, and Structures

Topics: a house with a strange shape, a bus converted into a home, an upside-down building, a floating structure.

General Template

You won’t believe what I’m looking at. [Where you are and what is odd].

It looks like [shape, size, colour, materials, surroundings]. On top of that, [one more strange detail].

That’s why [why it is unusual]. I wonder [a question about the reason].

5.1 Upside-Down House

A house built upside down with the roof on the ground. Describe it to someone on the phone.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. I’m at a tourist attraction, and there’s a whole house built upside down.

It looks like a normal two-storey cottage with white walls and blue shutters, but the roof is on the ground, and the chimney is pointing down into the grass. On top of that, the front door is at the top, and visitors enter through a staircase that leads up to a window. Meanwhile, inside, all the furniture is nailed to the ceiling, including the table, the chairs, and even the lamps.

That’s why you feel dizzy when you walk through. I wonder how the architects came up with the idea. It’s an optical illusion, and the kids love it.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
chimney a pipe that carries smoke from a fireplace Smoke came from the chimney.
nailed fixed with nails The sign was nailed to the wall.
optical illusion something that tricks the eyes It’s an optical illusion.

5.2 Beach Bus Café

A bus that has been turned into a cafe on a beach. Describe what you see.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. I’m on the beach, and there’s an old double-decker bus that has been turned into a café.

It looks like a bright red London bus, sitting on the sand, with its wheels half buried. The lower floor has a counter and a few stools, and a barista is making coffee through a window. On top of that, the upper deck has tables with umbrellas, and customers are enjoying drinks while looking at the ocean. Meanwhile, there’s also a hand-painted sign that says “Sunset Bus.”

That’s why it’s such a popular spot. I wonder how they got the bus onto the beach. It’s a very creative use of an old vehicle, and the view is stunning.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
popular liked by many people A popular café.
creative original and imaginative A creative design.
stunning extremely beautiful A stunning view.

5.3 House on a Rock

A house built on a very tall, narrow rock. Describe the situation.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. First of all, I’m on a hiking trip, and there’s a small house standing on top of a very tall, narrow rock.

It looks like a stone column, about fifty metres high, rising from the valley, and a tiny white house with a red roof sits right on the top. There’s no road, only a narrow wooden bridge and a thin metal ladder going up the side. On top of that, there’s a small flag on the roof, and a woman is hanging laundry outside.

That’s why it seems almost impossible. I wonder how they carried the building materials up there. It must be a breathtaking place to live, but definitely not for people who are afraid of heights.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
narrow not wide A narrow path.
breathtaking amazing A breathtaking view.
afraid of heights scared of high places I’m afraid of heights.

5.4 Boat in the Desert

A boat in the middle of a desert with people having a picnic on it. Describe what you see.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. First of all, I’m in the middle of a desert, and there’s a boat here, and people are having a picnic on it!

It looks like a medium-sized wooden fishing boat with peeling blue paint, lying on the hot yellow sand. A family of four is sitting on a checkered blanket on the deck, with sandwiches and a thermos. On top of that, there’s not a single drop of water for miles, only dunes and a few cacti in the background, and the father is pretending to steer the boat.

That’s why it looks like a scene from a dream. I wonder whether it’s an art project or a movie set. Either way, it’s completely surreal.

Vocabulary

Word Meaning Example sentence
thermos an insulated bottle for drinks A thermos of tea.
cacti spiky desert plants Cacti grow in deserts.
steer direct the course of a vehicle He steered the boat.
movie set a place built for filming A huge movie set.
surreal strange like a dream A surreal scene.

5.5 Book Phone Booth

A phone booth filled with books in a village. Explain what it looks like.

Sample

Hi, it’s me. You won’t believe what I’m looking at. First of all, I’m in a small village square, and there’s an old red phone booth full of books.

It looks like a traditional British telephone box, with glass windows and a black door, but the phone has been removed, and the inside is covered with wooden shelves. On top of that, there are about a hundred paperbacks, children’s stories, and travel guides, and a handwritten sign says “Take a book, leave a book.” A woman is standing inside, reading a novel.

That’s why it works as a mini community library. I wonder who started this lovely idea. It’s a clever way to recycle an old booth and encourage reading.

Vocabulary
